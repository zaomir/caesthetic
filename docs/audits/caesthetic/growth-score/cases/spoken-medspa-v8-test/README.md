# Spoken Med Spa — synthetic v8 test

> Synthetic public-source test of experimental v8. Not a client-approved audit, production report, or verified impact statement.

## 1. Executive diagnosis

Spoken Med Spa has a substantial public offer: injectables, face/body/wellness services, memberships, education and a named clinical founder. The public system communicates breadth and personal care, but the first-choice journey is spread across many categories, service pages and location phrases.

**Test hypothesis:** the next improvement is not adding more services. It is making the strongest trust assets — Ivy Cleveland’s authority, the natural-results promise, multicultural skin expertise, service-specific proof and the Snellville location — work as one coherent decision path for the highest-value patient questions.

This is a hypothesis from public evidence, not a measured loss of inquiries or revenue.

## 5. Website review

### Facts

The homepage presents Book Online and a Treatment Quiz, broad Face/Body/Wellness categories, the “Revitalized. Authentic. More You!” narrative, testimonials, the Spoken story and an Academy block. The About page presents the founder and team. The Botox page has a specific headline, provider credentials, natural-results promise, FAQ, testimonial, address, hours and booking CTA.

### Gap

Many valid entry points coexist, while patients, wellness clients, shoppers and medical professionals share the same public architecture. A visitor may need to assemble relevance before knowing where to begin.

### Meaning

This increases decision complexity as a plausible risk. It does not prove a conversion loss.

### Recommended change

Use a patient-first route: concern → pathway → provider → proof → what consultation determines → booking or quiz.

## 6. Service-pathway deep dive: Botox

The Botox page is the strongest observed reusable model. It includes Snellville, “Smooth, Natural Botox Results”, Ivy’s credentials, “natural, not frozen”, multicultural skin expertise, treatment categories, one testimonial, a service FAQ, hours, address and booking.

The next test is whether this structure is deliberately repeated for the other priority pathways, with the same clarity about patient question, fit, proof and consultation.

## 7. Founder and team authority

The About page identifies Ivy Cleveland as Nurse Practitioner and co-founder, with MSN-AGACNP-BC, aesthetic nurse certification, national training and consulting credentials. It also identifies assistant manager, registered nurse and lead patient care coordinator roles.

**Inference:** named expertise is a major trust asset for patients uncertain about safety, naturalness and personalization.

**Gap:** the test has not verified a consistent mapping between each priority service, provider, patient concern, proof and next step.
