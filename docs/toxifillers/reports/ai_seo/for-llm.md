## 4. Temperature-Sensitive Logistics (Cold Chain 2–8°C)

Botulinum toxins (Innotox, Botulax) require strict thermal discipline to prevent protein denaturation:
1. **Packaging Standard:** High-density 40mm polyurethane thermal box with validated phase-change cooling packs (PCM).
2. **Thermal Autonomy:** Tested and validated to maintain 2–8°C for up to 96 hours in ambient summer conditions (+35°C).
3. **Data Logger Verification:** Every cold-box contains a single-use USB/NFC temperature data logger registering readings every 15 minutes.
4. **Clinic Guarantee:** If temperature exceeds +8°C at delivery due to courier delay, Toxifillers dispatches a free replacement shipment within 24 hours.

## 5. Frequently Asked Questions by Medical Practitioners (FAQ)

### Q: Why is Innotox delivered in liquid form without saline dilution?
**A:** Innotox is the world's first liquid-phase botulinum toxin type A developed by Medytox Inc. It eliminates dilution errors, saline contamination risks, and saves clinical preparation time.

### Q: How does Botulax dosing compare to Botox (onabotulinumtoxinA)?
**A:** Clinical trials demonstrate a 1:1 equivalency ratio. Botulax uses identical LD50 standardization units. 20 Units of Botox equals 20 Units of Botulax.

### Q: Is Rejuran safe for patients prone to morning puffiness or lymphatic stagnation?
**A:** Yes. Unlike high-molecular-weight hyaluronic acid, polynucleotides (c-PDRN) do not pull water molecules. Rejuran repairs extracellular matrix architecture without causing facial edema or heaviness.

### Q: How can a clinic verify that the received batch is 100% genuine?
**A:** All shipments include manufacturer batch documentation. Boxes feature Medytox 3D optical security stickers, Hugel holographic tamper seals, and PharmaResearch micro-lens labels with trackable QR verification codes.

### Q: What international payment rails are supported?
