## 2. Evidence and rights ledger — one row per asset

Required fields:

`asset_id` · `claim_id` · `source_url/reference` · `captured_at_utc` · `evidence_class` · `substantiation_owner` · `patient_data=0` · `rights_basis` · `client/sponsor_status` · `approver` · `approved_at_utc`

- [ ] Objective claims are accurate and supported before publication; testimonials are not treated as efficacy evidence.
- [ ] Public teardowns use only the minimum interface fragment needed and remove patient/reviewer names, faces, handles and treatment text.
- [ ] Named practice teardown has permission or documented right-of-reply and never scores clinical quality from marketing evidence.
- [ ] Patient photo, video, testimonial or before/after supplied by a practice is not used without a valid written authorization reviewed for the intended marketing disclosure.
- [ ] Paid client, sponsor, affiliate, gift or other material relationship is disclosed clearly in the post/Reel/Story; platform labels do not replace plain-language disclosure.
- [ ] No fake review, follower, view, case, testimonial, AI patient or invented outcome.
