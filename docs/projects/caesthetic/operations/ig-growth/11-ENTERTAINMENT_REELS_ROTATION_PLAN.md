## 1. Corrected decision

The downloaded Reels are useful, but not mainly as a reach layer.

For CAESTHETIC Phase 1, entertainment is a controlled **owner-language and relatability instrument**:

1. collect owner-operator verbatim language from comments and replies;
2. test whether relatable hooks improve or poison distribution for later evidence content;
3. make the feed less exhausting without turning the account into staff comedy;
4. generate prompts for future Format A informational Reels and carousel topics.

Entertainment must not take capacity away from the informational Reel cycle.

## 2. Audience correction

CAESTHETIC Phase-1 ICP is A1:

- independent US med-spa / aesthetic practice owner-operator;
- normally one location;
- roughly `$50–150k/month` revenue range;
- signs the bill or controls the growth budget.

Therefore:

| Pool | Count | Status | Reason |
|---|---:|---|---|
| E3 owner-operator POV | 14 | **Working core** | Strongest fit. Speaks to budget, calendar, operations and growth pressure. |
| E0 broad medspa reach mechanic | 10 | **Working core** | Hook mechanics are portable if topic is rewritten into owner-side growth leaks. |

| E2 fictional front-desk conversation | 2 | **Quarantine / second wave** | Can blur with evidence formats on enquiry/no-show/price topics and may require actor timing. |

E1/E2 are not deleted. They are held until audience composition data proves they do not dilute owner reach.

### 2.1 Quarantine exit rule

After four weeks of E3/E0/E5-only entertainment/VOC activity:

1. manually review a sample of 30–50 new followers from the entertainment window;
2. classify each as owner/operator, staff/injector/esthetician, vendor, unclear or irrelevant;
3. compare owner/operator share with the pre-entertainment baseline and informational-only weeks.

If owner/operator share does not fall materially and neighboring informational Reels are not weakened, E1 may reopen partially, starting only with the least floor-specific, most owner-readable references.

E2 remains closed longer by default because enquiry, price and no-show skits sit closest to topics that Format A treats as evidence. E2 requires a separate founder GO, fiction/evidence visual treatment and actor/production lane before use.

## 3. Scheduling correction

The previous `one weekly Reel slot alternating informational/entertainment` plan is rejected.

Reason: it produces only two informational Reels per month and makes the informational Format B cycle physically impossible.

The informational Reel engine needs room for:

```text
Format A → Format A → Format A → Format B synthesis
```

If entertainment takes half the Reel slot, one A→A→A→B cycle stretches across roughly two months and cannot produce useful testing data.

## 4. Default schedule

Keep the canonical 3 feed posts/week, but do **not** put entertainment inside the main Reel slot.

| Day | Feed role | Format |
|---|---|---|
| Monday | Diagnostic / Growth Leak | Carousel |
| Wednesday | Evidence engine | Informational Reel, normally Format A; every 4th informational Reel becomes Format B synthesis |
| Friday | Public proof / city pattern / market evidence | Static, carousel or proof Reel depending on evidence availability |
| Tuesday Stories | Relatability + VOC capture | Open question sticker or owner prompt; no option-only poll as VOC |
| Thursday Stories | Relatability + VOC capture | Open question sticker or follow-up prompt |
| Optional Tuesday extra feed post | E5 conversation starter or E0/E3 faceless prompt | Maximum one per week, only if ready; never replaces Wednesday |

This preserves informational throughput while adding relatability in cheaper, lower-risk surfaces.

Hard substitution rule: entertainment does not fill Wednesday under any circumstance. If the informational Reel is not ready, publish a compliant cutdown from `derived_episodes` or skip the slot. Skipping is preferable to training the account toward the wrong audience.

## 5. If feed capacity expands

## 6. Production ownership and face policy

Valeriia should not be the default face of entertainment.

Canonical Valeriia register is calm authority, analytical, not influencer-style and not a sales presenter. No-show skits, receptionist dialogue or trend-comedy timing require a different production lane.

Allowed entertainment production modes:

| Mode | Fit | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Faceless typography + montage | Best for E0, E5 | Works with deterministic render; safest for brand. |
| Screen/UX satire | Best for E0/E3 | Show public journey friction without mocking patients or practices. |
| Second human/operator character | Possible later | Must define relationship to brand before use. |
| Valeriia as comedic actor | **Default no** | Only by explicit founder decision and new creative brief. |
| Synthetic comedy avatar | **No** | High failure risk for timing/trust; not aligned with Phase 1. |

Valerie/opening/avatar tests should not be judged from organic entertainment or from a handful of organic informational Reels. At one informational Reel per week, the A→A→A→B cycle yields only four Reels/month and cannot isolate avatar or opener variables. Valerie/opening tests belong in a paid layer or other controlled distribution test, not in organic inference from two posts.

## 7. Rights boundary

For references, borrow only:

- format;
- generic structure;
- edit rhythm;
- scene category;
- contrast mechanic;
- prompt type.

Do not copy:

- exact joke;
- exact punchline;
- exact spoken line;
- exact script sequence;
- creator caption phrasing;
- creator audio unless it is available and permitted for the CAESTHETIC business account.

Business-account audio must be checked before scripting. Many trend-audio mechanics will not be available to commercial profiles.

Entertainment content has a no-numbers rule. It must not contain numbers, percentages, rankings, benchmarks, response-time figures, cost examples, revenue examples or pseudo-statistical punchlines. Any content that needs a number exits entertainment and enters the evidence/claim compiler.

## 8. Evidence separation

Entertainment and evidence content must be visually separable.

For topics that overlap with Format A evidence work, such as slow enquiry response, pricing questions, no-shows and booking friction:

- entertainment uses clear labels like `FICTIONAL OWNER SCENARIO` or `RELATABLE PATTERN`;
- informational Reels use evidence labels like `OBSERVED`, `MEASURED`, `CALCULATED`, `ESTIMATED` or `ILLUSTRATIVE`;
- never present a skit as a measured Growth Score finding;
- never let a fictional scene cite a real practice, real patient or real staff member.

## 9. Measurement

Entertainment success is not judged primarily by reach.

Track three gates:

| Gate | How to measure | Decision rule |
|---|---|---|
| Audience composition | Manually review 30–50 new followers after each entertainment wave: owner/operator vs staff/injector/esthetician/vendor/unclear | If owner share falls materially, keep E1/E2 closed and reduce entertainment feed usage. |
| Neighbor effect | Compare informational Reel performance in weeks after entertainment vs weeks without it | If entertainment weakens owner-qualified reach or Growth Score actions, move it to Stories only or pause. |
| VOC output | Feed prompt: public comments. Stories: open-question DM replies. Poll taps do not count as verbatim. | Prioritize formats that generate owner/operator language, not generic likes. |

Primary entertainment KPI:

```text
usable owner/operator verbatim quotes per post
```

Secondary KPIs:

- owner-quality comments;
- qualified DMs;
- profile visits from owner/operator profiles;
- Growth Score clicks after neighboring informational content.

Views, likes and broad follower growth are diagnostic only.
