## No-camera assembly (canonical when nobody films)

Do **not** wait for Valerie, a studio, or a voice take. L5 and L7 ship as a **still-card Reel**. That is not a downgrade for this ICP: owners read.

### What “no shoot” means

| Allowed | Not a substitute |
|---------|------------------|
| Editorial text cards (1080×1920) from the locked VO lines | AI talking head / HeyGen / avatar |
| Public Maps/site **screenshots** (crop, redact names) | Beauty-spa stock, handshakes, fake dashboards |
| Labeled demo Growth Score stills (`/score/demo-*`, on-screen `ILLUSTRATIVE`) | Unlabeled “client report” |
| Soft Ken Burns (slow zoom 100→108%) | Fast TikTok zoom spam, trending-audio dependence |
| Silent + burned text, or Instagram/CapCut TTS of the **exact** VO | New improvised script, hidden LSI dump |

Screen-recording a public GBP is capture, not a shoot. If even that is too much — text cards alone are enough for L5/L7.

### Assembly (CapCut or Instagram “Create → Text / Photos”)

1. New 1080×1920 project, 24–32s.  
2. One card = one row from the shot list. Duration 3.5–4.5s.  
3. Card layout: CAESTHETIC mark + one sentence (the on-screen text) + optional screenshot in the lower half. Source Serif / IBM Plex if exporting from our template; otherwise Instagram default **large, high contrast, no pink/gold**.  
4. Hook card must be readable at 0:00 with sound off.  
5. Last card: `Free Growth Score` + `caesthetic.com/growth-score`.  
6. Export H.264, then paste the locked caption. Gate `08` still applies.
