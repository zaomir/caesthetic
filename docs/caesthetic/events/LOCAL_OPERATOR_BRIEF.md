## Scope requested from operator

Quote separately:

1. project / agency management fee;
2. venue sourcing and negotiation;
3. production management;
4. sound / light / stage / screens as required;
5. décor / furniture / branding;
6. catering coordination;
7. staffing / registration desk / host support;
8. photography / video if offered;
9. transport / guest logistics if offered;
10. event-day management;
11. teardown / settlement / vendor close;
12. contingency.

CAESTHETIC normally owns digital registration, ticketing logic, partner attribution and reporting.

## Optional partner sourcing

Ask the operator to state separately:

- whether they can originate sponsors/partners;
- category strengths;
- sourcing fee;
- whether compensation is fixed or a share of revenue they directly originate;
- requested protection period;
- proposed treatment of repeat events.

Preferred principle:

```text
base production fee
+ approved pass-through costs
+ explicit sourcing economics for operator-originated partners
```

Do not grant blanket ownership of a partner or a default share of all CAESTHETIC economics.

## Non-circumvention framing

The goal is to make the local operator safe from being bypassed without trapping CAESTHETIC in permanent partner ownership.

Recommended commercial concept:

- partner/source is recorded;
- operator receives the agreed sourcing compensation for the event it originated;
- a defined trailing rule may apply to repeat event-production participation for a stated period;
- unrelated future CAESTHETIC services or partner relationships are not automatically commissionable;
- CAESTHETIC does not bypass the operator for the same production opportunity merely to avoid the agreed fee.

Final wording requires contract/legal review.

## Quote comparison

Record:

- fixed fee;
- estimated direct production budget;
- catering included/excluded;
- minimum project budget;
- deposit schedule;
- cancellation exposure;
- supplier markups;
- partner sourcing terms;
- timeline;
- named producer;
- relevant comparable work;
- capacity for recurring events;
- Russian/English capability;
- references;
- exclusions.

## Operator score

Suggested decision dimensions, not automatic weights:

- premium-event quality;
- cost transparency;
- flexibility;
- local supplier network;
- venue access;
- sponsor/partner sourcing ability;
- speed;
- reporting discipline;
- willingness to work under CAESTHETIC event architecture;
- repeatability;
- problem-solving quality.

Cheapest quote is not automatically preferred. Select for expected event contribution and execution risk.
