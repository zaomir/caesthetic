# Bishkek Partner Outreach — 100 Account Status

This file tracks only the **additional 100-candidate universe**. Earlier Bishkek pilot outreach is a separate cohort and must not be counted as part of these 100.

## Campaign objective

**Contact all 100 distinct valid candidate organisations**, gradually and safely.

`HOLD`, `PENDING` or an invalid email address is **not** an account-level terminal state. It means: continue research and find another verified decision-maker, department mailbox or official route. A company may be permanently excluded only when it is demonstrably defunct, a duplicate, outside the Bishkek/target scope, or legally/suppression prohibited; the reason must be recorded.

## Sending contract

- Sender: `a@caesthetic.com`.
- Research before send: confirm current organisation, current reason for relevance and correct decision-maker role.
- Preferred recipient: verified personal work email for Marketing / Partnerships / Events / Business Development / Sales & Marketing / GM / owner.
- Fallback: verified official department/company mailbox with explicit routing/personalization to the named decision-maker.
- Never invent or pattern-guess an email.
- First touch: short plain text, one supportable relevance signal, one CTA, no attachment, no pricing, no speculative confirmed-partner claims.
- Stop company sequence immediately on reply.
- Do not send to invalid, pending, suppressed, complained, bounced or ambiguous addresses.
- Invalid route → research an alternate route on a later pass; do not mark the company complete.
- Weekday cadence: max 5 new companies per run, around 10:15 Bishkek time.
- Expansion gate: only raise above 5/day after a clean observation window; current automation cap remains 5.
- Deliverability stop: if recent hard-bounce rate for this cohort reaches **2% or more**, or any spam complaint / sender warning appears, pause all new sends and investigate.
- Git stores company/status/evidence state only; no personal email addresses or other PII.

## Reply handling

A substantive reply changes the account from outbound to human-owned dialogue. Classify as one of:

`interested / request_information / referral / wrong_person / not_now / commercial_question / compliance_question / negative / unsubscribe / unclear`.

Do not continue automated first-touch outreach to the same company after any substantive reply.

## Completion condition

The campaign reaches its primary completion condition when every one of the 100 distinct valid candidate organisations has received a first contact through a verified route, or has already replied before a new first touch was needed.

Non-terminal research states:

`QUEUED / RESEARCHED / RETRY_ROUTE / INVALID_ROUTE / PENDING_VERIFICATION`.

Terminal exceptions without a send are permitted only as:

`EXCLUDED_DEFUNCT / EXCLUDED_DUPLICATE / EXCLUDED_OUT_OF_SCOPE / EXCLUDED_SUPPRESSION_OR_LEGAL`,

with a durable reason recorded. An invalid mailbox alone never qualifies as an exclusion.
