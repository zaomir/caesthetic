# CAESTHETIC Events — Bishkek pilot

This file records **evidence-safe operating state** for the first market pilot. Do not store private contact data, attendee PII, direct personal email addresses, CRM exports or signed commercial terms here. Twenty/Gmail/runtime systems own live contact state.

## 1. Current objective

Build a sufficiently diverse pool of premium-audience holders, reach the correct decision makers, identify real interest, and form the first viable event cluster **before** locking a public event concept.

The pilot is discovery/formation, not yet an announced event.

## 2. Current partner categories in outreach

As of 2026-09-13, direct routing outreach has started across:

- premium automotive;
- premium fitness / sport;
- luxury / upper-upscale hospitality;
- international / private education;
- private banking / banking audiences;
- premium residential / development;
- business community / chamber.

Expert Dental remains available as the first Health/Smile participant but is not required in every future cluster.

## 3. Outreach operating rule

First contact asks only for the person responsible for partnerships, brand collaborations or joint events.

After referral / reply:

1. research the business and its audience;
2. send a category-relevant second message;
3. explain non-competing audience combination and partner-owned distribution;
4. do not request a customer database;
5. record interest and Partner Value Assets;
6. do not promise a named event, venue, celebrity, partner or reach until confirmed;
7. cluster only after real interest exists.

Direct Gmail is the preferred first-wave channel. LinkedIn is used to identify decision makers and for selective follow-up. The Instantly campaign created during setup remains non-authoritative for sending and must not be activated if it would duplicate direct Gmail outreach.

## 4. Confirmed operating findings

### Email

- Direct Gmail is workable for high-value account-first outreach.
- Generic published addresses may be distribution aliases rather than one mailbox; delivery evidence must be interpreted carefully.
- A syntactically plausible/publicly indexed school domain produced a hard delivery failure. Future sends require verification or strong official-source confirmation before use.
- A dedicated verification list is used as a safety check for new addresses; `pending` is not treated as verified.

### LinkedIn / decision makers

LinkedIn is useful as a decision-maker discovery layer, but current connector coverage is incomplete for some Bishkek professionals. Web/official evidence may identify a role when the connector has no result; do not infer that an old role is current without recency evidence.

Current high-confidence role patterns include:

- hotel: Director of Sales & Marketing / Business Development / General Manager;
- school: Head/Director plus admissions/community route when appropriate;
- chamber/community: Executive Director / Membership / Partnerships;
- bank: Marketing / Partnerships / Premium or loyalty-program owner;
- fitness: Marketing / Sales / Club management;
- automotive: Marketing / Brand / Commercial / General management.

## 5. Strong current signals

These are market signals, not partnership commitments:

- premium hotels in Bishkek actively operate event halls and client-event infrastructure;
- premium fitness clubs operate annual membership models and guest-visit inventory;
- at least one major bank has publicly participated in a premium fitness/Technogym event, supporting the hypothesis that banks may value lifestyle activations around affluent audiences;
- international schools and business chambers provide concentrated communities with relationship-based distribution, though school/family participation needs especially careful audience relevance and safeguarding;
- premium automotive dealers and developers are strong candidate audience holders but must be approached through brand/commercial owners rather than generic sales pitches.

## 6. First cluster readiness gate

Do not announce or book the first event until the cluster has:

- at least one credible anchor with confirmed interest;
- at least two complementary organizations with confirmed interest;
- no unresolved direct category conflict;
- explicit or realistically negotiable distribution commitment;
- a coherent attendee reason to come;
- one viable venue/host route;
- a local production route and preliminary cost range;
- registration/ticketing model;
- measurement/attribution plan;
- downside/cancellation cap.

## 8. Measurement baseline to capture from the first event

Before event launch define:

- invited/reported reach by partner and evidence type;
- tracked registrations by source;
- attendance by source;
- paid vs complimentary ticket allocation;
- partner-specific opt-ins;
- Partner Value Asset commitments and fulfilled value;
- direct production cost and pass-through costs;
- sponsor/participation/ticket collections;
- attributed partner sales where a signed PF agreement exists;
- repeat intent;
- partner and attendee complaints;
- local operator hours and budget variance.

Shipped ≠ Adopted ≠ Impact Verified.
