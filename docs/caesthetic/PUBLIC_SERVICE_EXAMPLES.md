## Website quality brief

Canon read: START, scoped ROUTER, PROJECT_ARCHITECTURE_STANDARD, PROJECT_DOMAIN_REGISTRY, project manifest/AGENTS/status, CAESTHETIC master/catalog, marketing/product authorities, WEBSITE_STUDIO_STANDARD, IMPECCABLE_WEBSITE_AGENT_STANDARD, DESIGN.md → CAESTHETIC_DESIGN_SYSTEM, design contract and canonical deploy authorities.

Surface mode: persuade. Thesis: Clinical Editorial Intelligence explains services through readable ruled rows and visible commercial boundaries. Character: precise, calm, accountable, readable. Anti-attributes: generic agency menu, dense SKU table, card/pill soup, decorative motion, fabricated proof. Existing typography, spacing and responsive components provide the visual reference; no new component family or media slot is needed.

Impeccable passes: clarify (concrete work), distill (curated projection), layout/adapt (existing narrow ruled lists), final audit/polish (hierarchy, keyboard, mobile and boundary visibility). Detector targets: the two modified page directories. Record tool results and visual review honestly; prose passes do not imply an unavailable slash-command tool ran.

Supporting guidance: [Google people-first content](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content) supports answering the audience's decision rather than creating thin search pages; [W3C heading guidance](https://www.w3.org/WAI/tutorials/page-structure/headings/) supports descriptive, nested headings for section navigation. These guide implementation and do not prove a business effect.
