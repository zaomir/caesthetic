## 0. Purpose

Instagram is the visual trust surface for CAESTHETIC Phase 1. Its job is not mass audience growth and not direct hard selling. The account should make an owner of an independent US aesthetic practice who arrives from manual warming, cold email, referral or organic discovery quickly conclude that CAESTHETIC understands where aesthetic practices lose patients and has a rigorous way to diagnose those leaks.

Primary path:

`ICP queue → manual Instagram warming → cold email with real observation → Instagram trust check → free Growth Score → recorded walkthrough → 30-Day Growth Sprint`

Primary CTA: **Get your free Growth Score.**

## 1. ICP and exclusions

Editorial ICP for the feed and offer: owner-operator of a single-location US med spa / aesthetic medicine practice, approximately **$50k–150k/month**.

Phase-1 **content** does not allocate feed slots to academy students, trainees, dental groups, generic beauty businesses, consumers seeking treatments, or procurement buyers. Older student/academy Instagram programs are superseded for Phase 1 unless a later founder decision explicitly reactivates them.

Outreach usernames are resolved from `docs/ssot/OUTREACH_USERNAME_REGISTRY.md` → `docs/ssot/data/outreach-username-registries.yaml` → `CURRENT.json`. This program does **not** define username qualification evidence gates (US geo website/GBP, patient-facing confirmation, `needs_qualification`). DEC-821.

The public product ladder is:

`Growth Score — free → 30-Day Growth Sprint — $2,500 → Growth System — optional later`

No required retainer to start. No contract beyond the 30-day Sprint. Do not use obsolete €10k+/month positioning.

## 2. Editorial thesis

CAESTHETIC is not presented as a generic marketing agency. The account should feel like a continuously operating **aesthetic-practice growth intelligence desk**.

Core idea:

> Patients don't experience your marketing as separate channels. They see one practice across Google, your website, social media, and reviews. CAESTHETIC makes those four surfaces work as one growth system.

The four decision surfaces are:
1. Search / Google Business Profile
2. Website
3. Social
4. Reputation / Reviews

**Cross-Surface Consistency Score** sits above these four surfaces; it is not a fifth surface. Consistency means service presence, positioning, proof and conversion paths tell a coherent story. It must never be reduced to keyword matching.

## 3. Content architecture

### P1 — Practice / market breakdowns — 35%
Public-data teardowns of real aesthetic practices and markets. Use observable facts first. Preferred recurring series: **Growth Score: [City]** and **Aesthetic Growth Index: [City]**.

Initial market research pool: Scottsdale · Nashville · Charlotte · Tampa · Raleigh · Austin · Naples · Charleston · Greenville.

A breakdown should normally answer: what is strong, what is weak, what competitors reveal, three priorities, and one thing not to spend on yet.

### P2 — Growth leaks — 25%
One observable failure point per asset: map visibility, review velocity, unanswered reviews, treatment positioning mismatch, booking friction, slow enquiry handoff, weak proof, or cross-surface inconsistency.

### P3 — Frameworks / methodology — 20%
Teach CAESTHETIC's operating model: Four Surfaces, Cross-Surface Consistency, Growth Score, Demand Map, Review Velocity, Booking Friction, Enquiry Response, Priority Stack, and Do Not Do.

### P4 — Proof — 10%
Public evidence, anonymized Growth Score excerpts, competitor comparisons and later verified client-system before/after evidence. Never invent clients, metrics, testimonials or attribution. A public practice analysis is not a client case unless CAESTHETIC actually performed the work and can substantiate the result.

### P5 — Product — 10%
Explain Growth Score, recorded walkthrough, Sprint scope, $2,500 price, fixed 30 days, client ownership of delivered materials, and optional Growth System. Product content should remain a minority of the feed.

## 5. Regular cadence

Baseline after the launch grid: **3 feed posts/week**.

Default weekly rhythm:
- Monday — diagnostic carousel / Growth Leak;
- Wednesday — Reel, normally 15–45 seconds, one idea and one evidence point;
- Friday — real practice / market breakdown or proof asset.

Stories: **4–5 weekdays/week**, normally 1–4 frames. Stories should behave like a live research desk: market being reviewed, anonymized/public observations, polls about what to inspect next, new Growth Score completed, or a useful fragment from the week's research.

Do not publish daily feed merely to hit volume. Reassess cadence after four weeks using actual account Insights and qualified funnel outcomes.

## 6. Format rules

- Carousels: 1080×1350, normally 6–10 slides; optimized for saves/shares.
- Reels: 1080×1920, normally 15–45s; hook immediately; burned-in captions; one thesis per Reel.
- Static: minority format, used for strong evidence statements or institutional identity.
- Stories: research desk / continuity, not lifestyle filler.
- Hashtags: 3–5 relevant maximum; no stuffing.
- Captions: English, US market language, evidence-first and specific.
- Link: bio/link sticker → `/growth-score/` as primary Phase-1 destination.

## 7. Voice and visual system

Voice: calm, analytical, specific, operator-side. Prefer an observation over a marketing claim. Prefer a number, screenshot, comparison or process artifact over an adjective.

Visual canon: **Clinical Editorial Intelligence** from `site-caesthetic/DESIGN.md` — premium industry journal + analytical report + B2B operating system.

Do not use beauty-spa pink, pseudo-luxury gold, generic agency stock imagery, AI-SaaS gradients, glowing UI, fake dashboards, or consumer treatment aesthetics.

## 8. Evidence and compliance

At least 80% of Growth Score/report-derived content should be observable Class A facts. Estimates must be explicitly labeled.

Forbidden:
- fake or AI-generated patient reviews;
- invented cases, logos, metrics, testimonials or results;
- guaranteed rankings, patient volume or revenue;
- patient before/after used as CAESTHETIC growth proof;
- reward-for-review;
- review gating or suppression of lawful criticism;
- suggested copy/paste patient review text;
- Toxifillers or injectable procurement/SKU promotion;
- consumer medical advice;
- academy/student content during Phase 1 without a new founder decision.

Review content may discuss neutral capture, response operations, review velocity and aggregate reputation intelligence.

## 9. Growth Score city series

The recurring editorial IP is **Growth Score: [City]**.

One research pass should feed multiple surfaces:

`market research → ranked practices → Growth Score observations → Instagram breakdown → cold-email observation → private Growth Score lead → later site insight if approved`

For each city, prefer 2–4 representative practices and extract one defensible market-level pattern. Never imply a statistically representative city benchmark unless the sample and method support that claim.

Every city asset must preserve source/evidence provenance internally.
