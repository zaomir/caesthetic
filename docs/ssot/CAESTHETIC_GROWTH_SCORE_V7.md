# Growth Score v7 — Evidence-led Location Alignment & Action Report

## 1. Canonical definition

**Growth Score v7** is a client-facing audit format for a single location or a
network of locations. It connects verified public evidence to a concrete
intervention plan:

> **Evidence → Problem → Priority → Fix → Verification → Offer**

V7 is a report and delivery contract, not a new scoring algorithm. It does not
replace the existing Growth Score evidence rules, Connect4 concept, or product
catalog. It defines how findings are presented so that the client can decide
what to correct first and how CAESTHETIC can implement it.

The central question is:

> Does the same location, offer and next step appear clearly and consistently
> across Search & Maps, Website, Social, and Reviews & Reputation?

## 2. What v7 is designed to achieve

The report must let the owner understand, without a presentation call:

1. what is publicly observable;
2. where information conflicts or disappears;
3. which patient or customer step becomes harder to understand;
4. what should change first;
5. what will be checked after the change; and
6. what fixed-scope implementation CAESTHETIC proposes.

The report is useful for a decision, not merely attractive. It must not promise
rankings, map positions, AI/LLM inclusion, review counts, ratings, leads or
revenue as an automatic result of consistency work.

## 3. Report topology

### 3.1 Single location

A single-location v7 report contains:

- a short decision summary;
- confirmed business and location identity;
- the Four-Surface evidence matrix;
- the patient/customer route to the next step;
- 3–4 prioritised gaps;
- measurable before → after definitions;
- a 30-day implementation plan;
- verification criteria and limitations;
- a fixed-scope offer.

### 3.2 Multi-location network (only for 2+ confirmed locations)

The network layer is eligible only when the business has **two or more
confirmed locations**. The count must be established from the intake and
publicly identifiable location evidence; a vague claim that the business is a
“network” is not enough.

A business with one confirmed location receives only the single-location v7
report. It must not receive a network parent, network comparison, repeated-gap
summary, or network-level conclusion. If a second location is discovered but
cannot be confirmed, keep it outside the network analysis and mark it as an
unknown.

A network report contains two linked levels:

1. **Network Audit** — repeated and shared patterns across the declared
   locations, with explicit coverage and limitations.
2. **Location Audit** — a full v7 report for one human-selected focus
   location.

The network page may link to additional location pages. It must not invent an
average Network Score, label a location “best” or “worst” without a defined
comparison, or imply that an observed issue exists at every branch.

For each finding in an eligible network, classify the scope as **network-wide**, **repeated**,
**location-specific**, or **not assessed**.

## 4. Evidence contract

V7 uses open/public sources only. Every material finding includes, where
available:

- a direct URL to the relevant page or profile;
- a quote, screenshot, number, or other inspectable artifact;
- the date checked;
- the affected location and surface;
- a confidence or limitation note.

Direct links must go to the relevant page or element, not simply to a home page.
No invented score, diagnosis, internal conversion claim, or unsupported
competitor conclusion may be published. Public paths may be inspected only up to
non-submission; no calls, forms, messages, bookings, CRM, analytics, patient
records, or other private systems are used as audit evidence.

## 5. Four-Surface consistency model

The required surfaces are:

| Surface | What is checked |
|---|---|
| **Search & Maps** | Name, category, services, address, phone, hours, location identity, map destination and local relevance. |
| **Website** | Location/service pages, blog, provider facts, proof, contact details and the next step. |
| **Social** | Profile identity, location, services, current content and the route to enquire. |
| **Reviews & Reputation** | Public reputation context, review themes, response quality, relevant platforms and the legitimate review-request process. |

The fifth operational layer is **Lead Intake**. It is not a fifth public
surface or a score. It checks whether a person can move from interest to a clear
enquiry or booking path. Internal intake defects remain unassessed unless the
required evidence and access are explicitly in scope.

The goal is semantic consistency, not copying the same text everywhere. Real
services, locations, people, claims and next steps must agree while each channel
uses its appropriate format.

## 6. Finding format

Each significant finding follows this sequence:

**Observed fact → Evidence → Why it matters → Exact correction → Verification**

Every correction has a measurable definition:

- **Before:** the currently observed state;
- **After:** the exact state to publish or configure;
- **Acceptance:** the direct check that confirms completion.

Example:

> **Before:** the location has different phone numbers on Maps and the website.
> **After:** one confirmed phone number appears on the selected Maps profile,
> location page, social profile and relevant reputation listings.
> **Acceptance:** all four URLs show the approved value and the enquiry path
> resolves to the same location.

## 7. Priorities and 30-day plan

The report manually selects a small number of material priorities rather than
turning every cosmetic issue into a sales argument:

1. one primary constraint;
2. two or three supporting gaps;
3. one explicit “do not fund yet” item when relevant;
4. a short list of safe, low-effort client fixes.

Each priority must map to a supported CAESTHETIC service/module, named assets,
dependencies, owner, acceptance test and realistic 30-day scope. If fewer than
three material problems are proven, v7 reports fewer; it does not manufacture
gaps to fill a quota.

## 8. Connect4 implementation layer

When the audit identifies cross-surface contradictions, the report links to the
English-only service page:

`https://caesthetic.com/connect4/location-alignment/`

The implementation aligns the selected location across:

- Search & Maps;
- location/service pages and blog;
- social profiles and selected posts;
- relevant reputation platforms and compliant responses to reviews.

The working unit is one location. Shared network facts and local exceptions are
kept separate. The client receives a before/after record and a repeat check,
not a promise of a particular ranking outcome.

## 9. Review Collection System

When appropriate, v7 may link to:

`https://caesthetic.com/connect4/review-system/`

The service is described publicly as CAESTHETIC’s own review and feedback
system. It routes customers through a tested process, supports legitimate
public feedback, and gives management a structured view of negative signals that
need attention. Internal routing, scoring and mechanics are not disclosed.

The system must not purchase reviews, incentivise a rating, gate access to a
public review, dictate review wording, or promise a particular rating or volume.
Negative feedback remains a legitimate input for service improvement.

## 10. Commercial close

The final section presents one clear next action. For the approved location-
alignment offer used in the ENT proposal:

- first five locations: **$500 per location**;
- sixth and subsequent comparable locations: **$300 per location**;
- the client may start with one location and review the completed work before
  expanding.

The amount is a proposed fixed-scope implementation price, not a guarantee that
all possible work at a location is included. The final scope, dependencies and
acceptance criteria are confirmed before work starts.

The **$500 Lead-to-Revenue Check** remains a canonical, separately purchased
recommendation in every Growth Score v7 report, whether the subject is a single
location or an eligible 2+ location network. It is voluntary and does not turn a
single-location report into a network analysis.

## 11. Required navigation and publication

V7 reports are published as website pages or controlled client links, not as the
primary PDF deliverable. The report should link directly to:

- the relevant location and evidence;
- the selected Connect4 service;
- the relevant article explaining the problem;
- the next report step or implementation offer.

The two Connect4 services remain English-only. Former `/ru/` service URLs
redirect to the English pages; no language switcher is added to those service
pages.

## 12. Version boundary

V7 is the action-oriented successor format for new audits that need explicit
cross-surface alignment and implementation. It preserves v6’s evidence,
human-approval and public-source boundaries. It adds the stronger **before →
after**, location-level implementation, verification and fixed-offer layer.

The 2+ confirmed-location rule is a hard format boundary: network analysis is
conditional, never a default wrapper around every v7 report.

It does not retroactively rewrite historical v6 reports, alter the scoring
algorithm, or make Connect4/Review Collection System a mandatory service for
every client.

## 13. Agent routing

Requests containing `v7`, `Growth Score v7`, `action audit`, `location
alignment audit`, `multi-location alignment`, or an audit that explicitly asks
for contradictions, before/after corrections and an implementation offer route
to this document after the mandatory Growth Score preflight.

The agent must still run the normal intake, public-research, evidence,
manager-approval, translation and publication gates. This SSOT determines the
report format; it does not waive those gates.
