# CAESTHETIC — Email → Instagram sequencing (Phase-1)

## 0. Decision

Instagram alone cannot cover the Phase-1 universe at follow pace. **Email carries volume; Instagram carries familiarity and trust.**

DEC-845 adds a mandatory boundary: **before an explicit Free Growth Score request, this lane uses verified account context and commercial signals — not an unrequested mini-audit, `1-Minute Leak`, full 4444 diagnosis or claimed binding constraint.**

Sequence for one practice:

```text
Day 0    IG touch (story-view ± like) on @caesthetic.growth where eligible
Day 1–2  optional second soft IG touch if Tier A
Day 2    Email #1 — verified context/signal + one question + Score CTA
Day 5    Email #2 — different non-diagnostic angle / signal / category insight
Day 9    Email #3 — soft break-up / leave door open
Anytime  follow-back → thanks DM (no cold link); email/social reply → stop sequence + human owner
```

Cold DM on Instagram remains **off**. Thanks DM after follow-back remains **on** under the current inbound/follow-up policy.

This document is the SSOT for the Email→IG lane. It does **not** unlock Instantly send without founder GO + deliverability check + qualification/suppression gates.

## 1. Jobs of each channel

| Channel | Job | Not the job |
|---------|-----|-------------|
| Instagram coverage | Be seen; create name familiarity | Clear the universe with follows |
| Instagram feed/Reels/Stories | Teach category + Score; earn trust/saves | Hard sell Sprint in first touch |
| Email (Instantly) | Scalable outreach with verified owner/practice email | Replace IG trust surface or deliver an unrequested audit |
| LinkedIn | Role/identity confirmation + professional reinforcement | Separate contradictory pitch |
| Meta ads | Later retargeting / validated B2B reach | Rescue an unvalidated funnel |

## 2. Universe and identity

- Candidate discovery data (including the current 1,441-account pool) is **research input, not send authority by itself**.
- Email universe: only qualified accounts with a **verified owner/practice email** after enrichment. Username alone is not an email.
- One company = one active opening narrative (`MARKETING_SYSTEM_STANDARD` / DEC-845).
- Pre-Score qualification answers at minimum: US? patient-facing? independent/local? website? owner/decision-maker?
- Tier A/B/C is based on ICP fit, signal/timing, economic potential, role confidence, data completeness and evidence confidence — not a pre-discovered leak.

## 3. Prerequisites before first Instantly send

1. Founder **GO** for Instantly volume.
2. Sending domains/inboxes warmed and deliverability checked; protect `caesthetic.com` from cold-sending reputation risk.
3. Account qualification passed under DEC-845.
4. Enrichment: account → practice/owner email from public/approved sources. **Do not invent emails.**
5. Verify every address via EmailVerifier.io SSOT before Instantly.
6. Suppression: do_not_contact, stop/complaint/unsubscribe, project conflict and applicable registry deny/suppression overlays.
7. Conflict: no simultaneous contradictory or aggressive cross-channel opening on the same company.
8. Pixel/paid readiness is separate; do not block approved email solely because paid attribution is not live.

## 4. Enrichment pipeline

```text
master/candidate account
  → AI qualification
  → owner / decision-maker enrichment
  → public work email discovery
  → staging / canonical contact update
  → EmailVerifier.io
  → suppression/conflict gate
  → Instantly list (verified only)
```

Privacy: no username/email row exports in Git. Private row-level data remains in canonical private storage/master systems.

Minimum Instantly fields: `email`, `first_name` (or practice), `city`, `state`, account/IG identifier where available, and one canonical narrative marker.

## 5. Email copy rules (plain text)

- Subject: verified context, signal or question — not “Free Growth Score!!!”.
- Body: normally 4–7 short lines.
- One verified context/signal.
- One question.
- One CTA to Free Growth Score / Score link.
- No attachment in first touch.
- No Sprint price or Calendly-first request in cold first touch.
- No Toxifillers / supply narrative in the CAESTHETIC US growth funnel.
- No fabricated case studies, fake compliments or invented findings.
- Same company never gets contradictory opening narratives across channels.

Acceptable pre-Score context examples:

```text
Saw you’ve added a second injector in Austin.
Saw the practice is actively promoting Morpheus8.
Saw you’re opening another location.
We’re reviewing independent aesthetic practices in Raleigh.
```

Forbidden before a requested Score unless the prospect explicitly gave permission for that specific evidence review:

```text
Your booking funnel is leaking.
Your reviews are costing you patients.
Your website is the binding constraint.
You are losing $X per month.
```

Example skeleton (EN):

```text
Subject: {{city}} practice — quick question

{{first_name}} —

I noticed {{verified_signal_or_context}}.

We run a complimentary Growth Score for independent aesthetic practices across Google, website, social and reviews to identify what should be fixed before more growth spend is added.

Would that be useful for {{practice_name}}?

— Valerie / CAESTHETIC
```

## 6. Instagram side of the sequence

| When | Action | Rule |
|------|--------|------|
| Day 0 | Story-view (± like if relevant recent post) | current coverage caps / CURRENT authority |
| Day 0–2 | Do **not** cold DM | cold DM OFF |
| If follow-back | Contextual thanks DM, no cold link blast | inbound/follow-up policy |
| During email window | Keep publishing Score/framework/evidence content | content calendar |
| If any substantive reply | Stop automated opening sequence | named human owner |

Do not require email enrichment before all permitted IG familiarity work, but both lanes must resolve the same account identity and suppression state.

## 7. KPI

Primary:
- activated qualified accounts;
- verified emails enriched;
- delivered / bounced / unsubscribed;
- positive replies;
- Score requests attributed to email/account cohort;
- Growth Scores delivered;
- qualified dialogues;
- paid Sprints per 100 activated qualified accounts.

Secondary:
- IG profile visits/follow-backs among emailed cohort (directional assist);
- channel-assist rate;
- human minutes per qualified dialogue;
- deliverability health.

Open rate is diagnostic, not a primary business outcome.

## 8. Forbidden

- Treat candidate/unqualified rows as send authority.
- Upload IG handles alone as a Meta Customer List.
- Buy followers, fake views, engagement pods, or gray “boost” farms.
- Cold DM the queue to “warm email”.
- Same generic blast copy across all cities/accounts.
- Sending before EmailVerifier/suppression pass.
- Pre-building a prospect-specific mini-audit / `1-Minute Leak` merely to justify the email.

## 9. Action plan

1. Keep qualification and signal scoring upstream of email enrichment.
2. Founder GO + deliverability readiness on sending domains/inboxes.
3. Enrich qualified Tier A/B accounts to owner/practice email; verify.
4. Build a 3-touch plain-text sequence using verified non-diagnostic context/signals + Score CTA.
5. Keep eligible IG familiarity coverage coordinated at account level; cold DM remains off.
6. Measure `signal → reply → Score request → delivered Score → dialogue → Sprint` by cohort.
7. Expand only after deliverability and downstream Score capacity remain healthy.
