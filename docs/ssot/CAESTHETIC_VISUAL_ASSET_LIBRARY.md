# CAESTHETIC Visual Asset Library

## Purpose

CAESTHETIC Reels are produced through a hybrid system:

Strategy → Script → Valerie scenes → Editor → B-roll → Motion → Captions → Final Reel.

HeyGen is the presenter layer. It is not the final editor.

The asset library exists to make every Reel feel like part of one operating system rather than a collection of AI videos.

## Core asset groups

### 1. Growth Score client view

Purpose:
Show what a practice owner receives.

Assets:
- Growth Score cover mockup.
- Four-Surface evaluation view.
- Search / Website / Social / Reputation sections.
- Constraint identification example.
- Priority stack.
- Do Not Fund Yet recommendation.
- Recorded walkthrough preview.

Rules:
- Use synthetic/demo data unless real client permission exists.
- Never imply fictional examples are client results.

### 2. Internal Growth Intelligence cockpit

Purpose:
Show how CAESTHETIC thinks.

Visual flow:

Evidence
→ Verification
→ Constraint
→ Priority
→ Intervention
→ Adoption
→ Impact

Assets:
- Research board.
- Evidence cards.
- Priority matrix.
- Intervention roadmap.
- Adoption checkpoint.

### 3. Four-Surface model

Purpose:
Explain that patients experience one practice across multiple surfaces.

Visual:

Search
Website
Social
Reputation

Above:
Cross-Surface Consistency

Rules:
Cross-Surface Consistency is not a fifth surface.

### 4. 30-Day Growth Sprint roadmap

Purpose:
Explain implementation.

Visual:

Week 1
Baseline + constraint

Week 2
Intervention

Week 3
Team adoption

Week 4
Measurement + handoff

Rules:
Activity is not the outcome. Adoption and impact require separate verification.

### 5. Client experience journey

Purpose:
Remove uncertainty.

Visual:

Submit Practice
→ Growth Score
→ Recorded Walkthrough
→ Optional Sprint
→ Day 30 Decision

Show three Day 30 paths:
- Continue internally.
- Use another provider.
- Continue with CAESTHETIC Growth System.

Valerie Petra is the canonical CAESTHETIC host.

Still photos (one Dropbox folder): `docs/ssot/CAESTHETIC_VALERIE_AVATAR_LIBRARY.md`.

Use:
- Editorial Strategy Office.
- Operations Desk.
- Strategy Room.
- Executive Meeting Room.

The environment should support the topic, not decorate the video.

## Editor handoff requirements

Every Reel package should include:

1. Valerie scenes.
2. B-roll requirements.
3. Mockup references.
4. On-screen text.
5. Caption style.
6. Timing map.
7. Claim verification notes.

## Forbidden visual patterns

Do not use:
- fake client dashboards implying real results;
- invented testimonials;
- generic spa stock footage;
- beauty glamour shots;
- fake revenue charts;
- AI neon interfaces;
- unsupported before/after claims.
