# CAESTHETIC IG — weekday Story templates (1 week)

**Surface:** `@caesthetic.growth`  
**Parent cadence:** `CAESTHETIC_IG_REACH_PLAYBOOK.md` (Stories 1–4 frames on weekdays)  
**Voice:** US med-spa / dental / injector **owner** English · research desk, not agency pitch  
**Default CTA:** free Growth Score (link in bio / Highlights) — **at most one CTA frame per day**; omit CTA on mention-loop days

## 0. Hard rules (every frame)

1. No clinical claims, ranking promises, patient/revenue guarantees, or “we will get you X bookings.”
2. No patient faces, PHI, private DMs, or unlicensed before/after.
3. Mention-loop frames: **no Score URL**; one factual line + `@handle` only.
4. Safe zones: keep primary text in the center third (away from username / reply bar).
5. Stickers: poll / quiz / question are optional; max one interactive sticker per day.
6. Publish on US business hours when possible (reach playbook §6).
7. Visual baseline: brand tokens / still cards from `CAESTHETIC_VISUAL_ASSET_LIBRARY.md` when available; otherwise calm dark or soft neutral full-bleed + large type. No purple-glow AI slop.

**Frame = one Story slide.** Rotate cities/patterns; do not invent named client results.

---

## Day 1 — Micro-diagnosis (3 frames)

**Job:** Make an owner recognise cross-surface inconsistency.  
**Maps to:** Four surfaces (`CAESTHETIC.md` §2) · carousel Q “same offer, different story.”

| # | On screen | Burned-in / caption text | Notes |
|---|-----------|--------------------------|-------|
| 1 | Full-bleed quiet desk / abstract grid; small wordmark | **Where practices lose patients** *(not clinical quality)* | Opening. No CTA. |
| 2 | Simple 2×2 or vertical stack labels: **Search · Website · Social · Reviews** with one red “mismatch” mark on Social or Website | **Same offer. Four different stories.** | Observable pattern only. |
| 3 | Soft card + bio arrow cue | **Free Growth Score → link in bio** | Only CTA of the day. Optional: “Highlights → START” |

**Optional sticker (frame 2):** poll — *“Do your Google + IG say the same offer?”* Yes / Not sure.

---

## Day 2 — Reel clip recycle (2 frames)

**Job:** Warm visibility from already-approved FAQ Reel; no new film day.  
**Maps to:** Reach playbook §1.1 · OWNER_MARKETING_QUESTIONS §8 (any shipped Reel 1–10).

| # | On screen | Burned-in / caption text | Notes |
|---|-----------|--------------------------|-------|
| 1 | 12–15s crop of approved Reel (Valerie or still-card path); captions already burned in | Keep Reel’s own line; add tiny top label **FAQ** | Prefer Reels 2, 5, or 6 for mid-funnel. |
| 2 | Still end-card matching Reel topic | **Full answer in Highlights → SCORE** *(or reel topic name)* | Soft CTA; no hard sell. |

**Do not** add a second CTA sticker if frame 2 already points to Highlights.

---

## Day 3 — Field note (3 frames)

**Job:** Pattern recognition without naming a real practice.  
**Maps to:** Owner questions on enquiry lag / ads vs system (Q03, Q05, Q11 family).

| # | On screen | Burned-in / caption text | Notes |
|---|-----------|--------------------------|-------|
| 1 | Map-ish abstract or city-neutral texture (no fake pin on a real clinic) | **Saw this again in Phase-1 medspas…** | “Phase-1” = our research set, not a client claim. |
| 2 | Three short bullets as large type (one pattern only per week) | Pick **one**: · *Reviews live — replies thin* · *Site traffic — weak next step* · *Reach up — bookings flat* · *Ads first — system unchecked* | One pattern. No numbers as “your loss.” |
| 3 | Calm close | **First fix ≠ more ads.** | Optional tiny: *Score ranks what not to fund yet.* |

**Optional sticker (frame 2):** quiz — *“When reach rises and bookings don’t, check first?”* Ads budget / Booking path / New logo.

---

## Day 4 — Product without hard sell (3–4 frames)

**Job:** Explain Score outcome + independence (objection removal).  
**Maps to:** OWNER_MARKETING_QUESTIONS §6 + Reel topics 2 / 4 / 10 framing.

| # | On screen | Burned-in / caption text | Notes |
|---|-----------|--------------------------|-------|
| 1 | Report mock / Score cover still (synthetic, labeled if needed) | **What you get after the Growth Score** | Product education day. |
| 2 | Checklist still | **Strongest surface · Binding constraint · Demand leaks · Do not fund yet** | Mirror Score deliverable language. |
| 3 | Simple ownership card | **Use it in-house. Or with any provider. No retainer required to start.** | Independence — Reel 4 spirit. |
| 4 *(optional)* | Bio cue | **Start free → link in bio** | Skip if the week already had 2+ Score CTAs. |

Never imply EHR login or PHI is required for the free Score.

---

## Day 5 — Mention loop **or** desk behind-the-scenes (1–2 frames)

### Path A — Mention loop (preferred when ≥1 follow-back in last 24h)

**Job:** They see *us*; reciprocity after warm follow-back.  
**Maps to:** Reach playbook §2.4 / §8. Cap **3–5/day** across the account, not only this template.

| # | On screen | Burned-in / caption text | Notes |
|---|-----------|--------------------------|-------|
| 1 | Their **public** feed post or Story (reshare / screenshot with clear public context) + `@theirhandle` | One factual line only, e.g. **Clear offer on the first screen.** / **Strong before-frame; CTA is soft.** | **No Score link. No “DM us.”** |
| 2 *(optional)* | Brand end card without URL | **Research desk notes — not a pitch.** | Skip if frame 1 is enough. |

If no eligible public post: do **not** force a mention; use Path B.

### Path B — Desk BTS (fallback)

| # | On screen | Burned-in / caption text | Notes |
|---|-----------|--------------------------|-------|
| 1 | Sanitized Score / GBP teardown still (no PII, no identifiable patient) | **Research desk, not a content farm.** | Presence only. |
| 2 *(optional)* | Highlight menu still | **START · SCORE · FAQ** | Point to Highlights; CTA optional. |

## Weekly reuse

| Week | Rotate Day 3 pattern | Prefer Day 2 Reel order |
|------|----------------------|-------------------------|
| W1 | Reviews / replies | Reel 5 → 2 |
| W2 | Website next step | Reel 6 → 1 |
| W3 | Reach vs bookings | Reel 2 → 4 |
| W4 | Ads vs system | Reel 3 → 7 |

Repeat the five **day jobs**; swap copy and assets. Do not invent a sixth daily format until these ship for two clean weeks.

## Agent / ops routing

- **Publish owner:** human or approved ops — default **manual** for mention frames.
- **Dolphin coverage runners** do **not** auto-post these templates.
- Clip pipeline for Day 2: HeyGen APPROVED Reel → editor crop → Stories 2–3 days (`CAESTHETIC_IG_REACH_PLAYBOOK.md` §1.1).
- After two weeks: note story reach, profile visits, replies in coverage ops report — then adjust CTA frequency, not follow caps.
