# CAESTHETIC Events & Partnerships

## 1. Decision

**CAESTHETIC Events & Partnerships** is a separate commercial direction inside the CAESTHETIC knowledge domain.

Its purpose is not to find advertisers for one clinic. It creates curated events and partner combinations where independent businesses with similar customer quality but non-competing offers bring their own audiences into one shared experience.

**Events is the customer-facing direction. CPRP / Twenty is the partner, attribution and settlement engine behind it.**

This does not create a new runtime unit and does not replace the canonical CAESTHETIC headline funnel `Free Growth Score → 30-Day Growth Sprint → optional Growth System`. Events & Partnerships may be sold under a separate SOW/capability when relevant and may also be used as a Growth System capability or an evidence-backed Sprint intervention.

Expert Dental Studio is the first pilot participant, not the owner or permanent center of the model.

## 2. Core model

Canonical loop:

```text
Premium Partner Universe
→ correct decision maker
→ permission / routing contact
→ partner interest
→ partner asset map
→ compatible partner cluster
→ event concept
→ audience/distribution commitments
→ local production
→ registration / ticketing
→ event delivery
→ attributed partner outcomes
→ settlement
→ repeat / new cluster
```

CAESTHETIC does **not** require a partner to transfer its customer database. The partner invites its own customers/followers through its own channels. A person enters the CAESTHETIC event flow only by taking an affirmative action such as registration, ticket purchase or another permissioned event enrollment.

## 3. Event-first principle

Do not design a fixed event and then force companies into it.

Default order:

1. build a pool of relevant audience holders;
2. contact the correct partnerships / marketing / events / commercial decision maker;
3. identify who is interested;
4. map what each interested party can contribute and what audience they want to reach;
5. form compatible clusters;
6. design a unique event around the strongest cluster.

A single interested company is an **Event Building Block**, not yet an event.

## 4. Partner roles

A partner may hold one or several roles:

| Role | Typical contribution |
|---|---|
| Audience Partner | invites its customers, members, residents, parents, clients or followers |
| Experience Partner | creates a useful or memorable part of the experience |
| Host Partner | venue, hospitality, location, logistics or space |
| Value Partner | membership, service, gift, benefit, prize or other value asset |
| Talent / Audience Magnet | trusted personality, expert, creator, host or community leader who contributes attention and trust |
| Sponsor | pays for explicit sponsor inventory and agreed deliverables |
| Local Event Operator | local production, vendors, venue execution and event-day management |

One event normally avoids direct competitors inside the same category unless the format explicitly requires comparison or multi-brand participation.

## 5. Partner Value Asset

Do not reduce partner economics to cash or clinic memberships.

**Partner Value Asset** is the canonical umbrella for a contribution that has measurable value to the event or other partners.

Examples:

- access to an audience through partner-owned distribution;
- memberships / subscriptions / benefit certificates;
- venue or hospitality;
- prize inventory;
- products or services;
- talent / expertise / speakers;
- content production;
- transport or mobility;
- media inventory;
- event staff or production resources;
- customer experience inventory.

An Expert Dental membership is one possible Partner Value Asset. It may be used as a funded benefit, prize, employee/VIP benefit or negotiated exchange asset. It is not a universal currency and does not imply that all future partners must have memberships.

## 6. Membership capability

Where a relevant business does not have a usable membership/subscription/benefit product, CAESTHETIC may sell **membership productization and implementation** as a separate capability.

Minimum contract before build:

- buyer and customer JTBD;
- product entitlement;
- funding / liability owner;
- price or complimentary rule;
- issuance and redemption logic;
- service delivery capacity;
- financial and legal treatment;
- attribution boundaries;
- expiration / refund / transfer policy;
- adoption and impact criteria.

A membership is introduced only when it solves a real partner/business problem; it is not mandatory event inventory.

## 7. Bishkek pilot strategy

Bishkek is the first operating market for this model because CAESTHETIC can test the system with Russian-speaking partners and has an existing pilot relationship with Expert Dental.

Expert Dental contributes three unusually useful pilot conditions:

1. a client-specific result-linked compensation model can be reconciled to attributed collected sales under the signed authority;
2. CAESTHETIC has practical access to decision makers and clinic administrators needed to validate handoff and delivery;
3. dental memberships can be tested as a Partner Value Asset for benefits, prizes and negotiated exchanges.

These advantages belong to the pilot. They do not become universal pricing or product assumptions.

## 8. Partner discovery — Bishkek

Initial target universe prioritizes organizations that already aggregate a premium or internationally oriented audience and have an economic reason to expand that audience.

Priority categories:

- premium automotive;
- premium fitness / wellness;
- private and international schools;
- private banking / affluent banking segments;
- premium residential / developers;
- luxury / upper-upscale hospitality;
- golf / sport / private clubs;
- business communities and chambers;
- premium travel / concierge;
- beauty / wellness / private healthcare;
- fashion / jewelry / premium retail;
- high-trust creators, hosts and public personalities.

Selection is based on **Distribution Power**, not prestige alone:

```text
audience relevance
× ability to reach that audience
× trust
× willingness to activate
× non-competing fit
× measurable contribution
```

Reported followers, customer counts or reach remain self-report/third-party estimates until observed through tracked registration or other evidence.

## 9. Outbound sequence

All outreach inherits `OUTBOUND_LED_DEMAND_GENERATION_STANDARD.md`.

Default first touch to a generic inbox is only a routing question: who owns partnerships, brand collaborations or participation in joint events.

Do not disclose a speculative event concept before there is a relevant decision maker and enough context.

After the correct person responds, the second touch is personalized to that business:

- state that CAESTHETIC organizes curated Bishkek events;
- explain that other partners come from non-competing categories with comparable premium audiences;
- explain that partners invite their own audiences and do not hand over customer lists;
- describe the specific value for that partner category;
- ask whether the format is relevant;
- only then design event concepts around the interested cluster.

Do not say the model is untested, improvised or the first attempt. Do not invent completed events, confirmed participants, audience size or results that do not exist.

Email sends require the canonical suppression and verification controls. LinkedIn is primarily a decision-maker discovery and selective follow-up channel; any write automation must follow the LinkedIn control plane and account registry.

## 10. Event Matching Engine

Twenty / CPRP should represent enough structured information to form partner clusters without pretending that an algorithm alone decides the event.

Minimum partner-clustering fields:

```text
organization_id
category
audience_type
audience_geography
audience_quality_signal
distribution_channels
reported_reach
observed_registrations
possible_partner_value_assets
commercial_objective
preferred_event_formats
venue_asset
content_asset
exclusivity_or_conflicts
interest_state
decision_maker
next_action
```

Compatibility is evaluated across:

- audience overlap by quality, not identical products;
- category conflict;
- complementary value;
- distribution capacity;
- venue / experience fit;
- economics;
- operational feasibility;
- brand/reputation risk.

CAESTHETIC human ownership remains accountable for final event composition.

## 11. Event readiness

Do not run an event merely because a calendar slot exists.

A cluster becomes ready when there is sufficient evidence for:

- at least one credible anchor;
- enough complementary participants to create a worthwhile experience;
- explicit distribution commitments;
- a defined audience and reason to attend;
- a viable venue / production route;
- registration / consent flow;
- budget and downside cap;
- clear partner inventory and obligations;
- measurement and settlement rules.

A practical default target for the first Bishkek pilot is one anchor plus two or more complementary organizations, but the event brief may justify another composition.

## 12. Ticketing and registration

Tickets can be:

- complimentary / invitation-only;
- paid general admission;
- paid VIP;
- funded by a partner;
- hybrid, with partner allocations plus public paid inventory.

Ticket revenue is a possible CAESTHETIC revenue line when contractually defined. It is not mandatory for every event.

Registration must preserve:

- event/campaign source;
- invitation partner source where known;
- permission scope;
- attendance state;
- partner-specific interest/opt-in;
- payment state if ticketed.

A registration for one event is not blanket consent for every future partner or client.

## 13. Economics

Potential CAESTHETIC revenue lines are independent and must not be double-counted:

1. event strategy / organization / management fee;
2. sponsor packages;
3. exhibitor / participation packages;
4. ticketing margin or ticket revenue where CAESTHETIC is the contractual seller;
5. client-specific attributed-sales performance fee where signed, measurable and reconciled;
6. share / fee from membership sales where specifically agreed;
7. membership productization / implementation;
8. setup and ongoing partner-program management;
9. event content production package;
10. permissioned post-event CRM / activation work;
11. white-label event production for a partner/client;
12. relevant CAESTHETIC marketing, Connect4, reputation/review or growth products sold separately when a real need is established.

Do not turn a partner conversation into an unrelated service catalogue. Marketing/reputation products are cross-sell opportunities only after relevance is evidenced.

## 14. Performance fee architecture

Twenty may project attributed commercial outcomes for every participating organization that signs a result-linked agreement.

Canonical lineage:

```text
Event
→ Invitation Partner / Campaign
→ Registration
→ Attendance
→ Provider Interest / Opt-in
→ Offer / Opportunity
→ Purchase / Collected Revenue
→ Attribution Decision
→ Eligible Fee
→ Reconciliation
→ Settlement
```

Rules:

- each payment has one source of financial truth;
- each fee has a signed basis and measurement window;
- event-assisted is not automatically event-sourced;
- one payment cannot create overlapping fee bases without an explicit allocation rule;
- returns/refunds create reversals;
- Twenty is the operating CRM/projection, not the sole financial source of truth;
- no partner gains access to another participant’s full customer pipeline.

## 15. Local Event Operator model

Because CAESTHETIC may operate remotely from Bishkek, local production is normally delegated to a local Event Operator.

CAESTHETIC owns:

- event strategy;
- partner architecture;
- commercial model;
- registration/ticketing logic;
- CRM/attribution design;
- sponsor inventory;
- measurement and settlement.

Local Event Operator may own:

- venue sourcing/negotiation;
- local vendors;
- décor / sound / light / catering coordination;
- staffing;
- production schedule;
- event-day execution;
- local operational troubleshooting.

Preferred compensation can combine:

- base production/project fee;
- pass-through approved costs;
- sourcing fee for partners they originate;
- limited trailing fee for repeat event participation of an agency-originated partner;
- optional share of sponsor/participation revenue created by partners they sourced.

The operator does not receive a default share of ticket revenue, client treatment/service revenue, membership revenue or all future CAESTHETIC business.

Do not promise permanent ownership of a partner. Instead protect the operator from circumvention on the event-production opportunity it sourced, with a defined scope and period.

## 16. Audience Magnets

A celebrity, creator, host, expert or community leader may participate as a **Talent / Audience Magnet**.

Possible value exchange:

- complimentary participation;
- content / professional production;
- access to a high-quality audience;
- co-branding;
- hospitality or partner benefits;
- paid talent fee where justified;
- agreed revenue share on tickets attributable to their campaign where contractually appropriate.

Follower count alone does not justify selection. Track invitation source → registration → attendance → downstream engagement.

## 17. Success and kill criteria

Pilot success is not `event shipped`.

Track separately:

- partners contacted;
- correct decision makers reached;
- interested partners;
- partner activation / distribution commitments;
- registrations by source;
- attendance by source;
- event contribution margin;
- sponsor/participation/ticket revenue;
- partner-specific qualified interest;
- attributed collected sales;
- reconciled CAESTHETIC fees;
- repeat intent;
- operator hours / budget variance;
- participant experience / complaints;
- whether partners repeat without Expert Dental.

The model becomes meaningfully independent from the clinic only when CAESTHETIC can produce a viable event or partnership cluster in which Expert Dental is not required.

Pause or redesign when repeated events show weak partner distribution, poor attendance, negative contribution, excessive manual coordination, low repeat intent or unreliable attribution.

## 18. Evidence boundaries

Distinguish:

- **Fact** — verified public/internal evidence;
- **Self-report** — partner-provided number or statement;
- **Inference** — reasoned interpretation;
- **Hypothesis** — to test;
- **Recommendation** — proposed action;
- **Verified impact** — measured result under an agreed baseline/attribution rule.

Do not claim an audience is affluent merely because a venue or brand is positioned as premium. Use observable proxies and then measure actual registration, attendance, purchase and repeat behavior.

## 19. Canonical relationship to CPRP

`CAESTHETIC_PARTNER_REVENUE_PLATFORM.md` remains the authority for partner identity, program units, Twenty/CRM boundaries, attribution/ledger and settlements.

This file owns the higher-level event-led business direction and partner-cluster operating model.

If the two conflict:

- financial lineage / CRM / settlement → CPRP authority;
- event-led product direction / partner discovery / clustering / local operator model → this file;
- CAESTHETIC positioning and headline funnel → `CAESTHETIC.md`.
