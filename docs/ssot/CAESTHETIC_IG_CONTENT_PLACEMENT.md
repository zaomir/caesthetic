# CAESTHETIC IG — content placement & amplification

**Surface:** `@caesthetic.growth` · Dolphin `833304152`  
**Companion (reach levers detail):** `docs/ssot/CAESTHETIC_IG_REACH_PLAYBOOK.md`  
**Companion (weekday Story copy):** `docs/ssot/CAESTHETIC_IG_STORY_TEMPLATES_WEEK.md`

This file is the SSOT for two related jobs discussed 2026-08-14:

1. **Placement** — what we publish, where, and how often.  
2. **Amplification** — how more of the Phase-1 audience *sees* those publications (organic first; paid only under locked gates).

Coverage runners (AM/PM) make *us* appear in *their* story viewers. Placement + amplification answer the opposite: they open *our* profile / Stories / Reels / feed.

## 0. Locked decisions

| Decision | Rule |
|----------|------|
| Follow is not a reach lever | Keep outbound follow **5–8/day**. Do **not** raise follows to buy post views (DEC-822 / DEC-824). |
| When follow may rise | Only after 7–14 days, **0** action blocks, follow-back **≥10–12%**, coverage stable — then **8–10**, never a blast. |
| Daily presence | **Own Stories 1–4 frames** on weekdays — not a brand-new Reel every day. |
| Reel library | **3–5 Reels/week** from the canonical FAQ set (`CAESTHETIC_OWNER_MARKETING_QUESTIONS.md` §8). |
| Volume channel | **Email carries volume; IG carries familiarity** (`CAESTHETIC_EMAIL_TO_IG.md`). |
| Forbidden amplification | Bought followers/views, pods, gray boost farms, IG-handle Meta Customer Lists, second IG on the same fingerprint as `833304152`. |

### 0.1 What “Stories 1–4 frames” means

One **frame** = one Story slide (still, screen, short clip, or text overlay).  
**1–4 frames** = publish that many Stories in a row on a weekday so warm viewers see motion in the story ring — without requiring a new Reel from scratch.

## 1. Placement — what goes where

| Surface | Cadence | Job | Canon pack |
|---------|---------|-----|------------|
| **Stories** | Almost every weekday, 1–4 frames | Warm visibility / research-desk presence | `CAESTHETIC_IG_STORY_TEMPLATES_WEEK.md` |
| **Reels** | 3–5/week | Discovery, saves, long-lived library | OWNER_MARKETING_QUESTIONS §8 + HeyGen pipeline |
| **Feed carousel / static** | Per launch grid | Save/share hooks; **3 pins** always live | Growth program §4 + reach playbook §4 |
| **Feed video (non-Reel)** | Rare | Usually weaker discovery than Reel | — |
| **Highlights** | Always on | START / SCORE / FAQ (owner-named) | Growth program |
| **Profile grid** | Fresh top post **&lt;24–48h** when coverage is heavy | Convert coverage touch → “saw our post” | Reach §2.5 |

### 1.1 Correct understanding (from strategy chat)

We need **daily Stories**, not necessarily a new Reel every day. Reels are a **library** produced via:

```text
Topic (§8 FAQ)
  → script → HeyGen Valerie scenes
  → editor (B-roll / Score mockups / captions)
  → optional still-card path
  → founder APPROVED
  → publish in US hours
  → clip → Stories for 2–3 days
```

Human work = fact check + APPROVED — not daily greenfield filming.

### 1.2 Weekday Story jobs (placement pack)

| Day | Job | Frames |
|-----|-----|--------|
| 1 | Micro-diagnosis (lose patients / same offer different story / soft Score CTA) | 3 |
| 2 | Reel clip recycle | 2 |
| 3 | Field note (one pattern) | 3 |
| 4 | Score outcome / independence | 3–4 |
| 5 | Mention loop **or** desk BTS | 1–2 |

Full on-screen copy: `CAESTHETIC_IG_STORY_TEMPLATES_WEEK.md`.

**Micro-diagnosis Day 1 beat (canonical short copy):**

1. *Where practices lose patients (not clinical quality).*  
2. Google vs Website vs Instagram — *Same offer. Different story.*  
3. Soft CTA — *Free Growth Score → link in bio* (not every day of the week).

Same narrative may ship as **3 still Stories** or as one **18–22s vertical video** (9:16) with the same three beats; claims rules identical (no clinical guarantees, no patient faces, no hard sell).

### 1.3 US publish timing (placement clock)

- Target: **America/New_York 09:00–11:00** and **12:30–14:00** weekdays.  
- Do not schedule by VPS local clock without converting to US Eastern.  
- Coverage timers (touch *their* accounts): **13:00 / 16:30 UTC** EDT-season proxy — see coverage ops. After US fall-back, shift UTC +1h.  
- Detail: reach playbook §5.

## 2. Amplification — how more people see *our* posts

**Principle:** more visibility of our posts = **own Stories + fresh grid + save/Reel hooks + warm re-touch + Email when GO** — not more follow requests.

Coverage today mostly *looks at them*. Amplification makes them look at *us*.

### 2.1 Organic levers (priority order)

| # | Lever | What to do | Why it amplifies placement |
|---|--------|------------|----------------------------|
| 1 | Profile conversion after touch | 3 pins live; fresh feed &lt;24–48h; clear Highlights | Story-view/like → profile visit → sees our post |
| 2 | Own weekday Stories | 1–4 frames daily (templates) | Followers / recent engagers see the ring |
| 3 | Mention / re-share loop | After follow-back: our Story with their **public** post + one fact; `@them`; **no Score URL**; cap 3–5/day | Pulls them (and sometimes their audience) back to us |
| 4 | Point collab | Tier A + warm only; ~1 city/week; observation-first | One collab often beats 50 follows |
| 5 | Save / share hooks | Captions & end-cards from reach playbook §4 (50 ideas); CTA Save / Send to ops lead | Algorithm favors saves/shares over likes |
| 6 | US-timed publish | ET windows above | Owners see content in their workday |
| 7 | Warm segment pressure | Prefer re-touch of follow-backs / engagers over flat one-pass of full queue | Quiet profiles rarely see our feed |
| 8 | Fact-comment (gated) | Drafts on; send after QA; Tier A; no links / no Score pitch | Soft pull to profile when enabled |
| 9 | Email → IG | After Instantly GO; enrich+verify; IG = familiarity | Main **volume** of intentional profile visits |

Detail for each lever: `CAESTHETIC_IG_REACH_PLAYBOOK.md` §2–§6.

### 2.2 Warm audience segmentation (amplification focus)

| Layer | Amplification action | Expectation |
|-------|----------------------|-------------|
| Follow-back / engaged | Own Stories + reply / mention | Highest chance they see us |
| Story ring + recent post | Like + (later) fact-comment | Medium chance of profile visit |
| Quiet profiles | Story-view only | Cheap touch; low chance they see our feed |

Do not treat the full ~1.4k candidate queue as equal for “see our post.”

### 2.3 Tricks underused at strategy time (still valid)

1. Daily own Stories (fastest “they see us” lever).  
2. Story-reply / mention after follow-back.  
3. Save-bait in posts, not only diagnostic beauty.  
4. Single-thesis Reels 15–30s (often stronger cold discovery than carousels).  
5. Re-touch warms after 3–5 days (story again), not only one list pass.  
6. Publish on US calendar.  
7. Email as delivery of eyes to a post (when founder GO).

### 2.4 Paid / gray amplification (усиление размещением за деньги)

| Path | Status |
|------|--------|
| Meta Boost / Ads | **Allowed later only** after Pixel ID + founder DEC + daily cap |
| Gray view/follower farms, pods, rented engagement | **Forbidden** |
| Upload IG `@handles` as Meta Customer List | **Forbidden** (Meta matches email/phone/MAID). Path = enrich → verified email → Customer List after GO/compliance |
| Second IG on same Dolphin fingerprint as `833304152` | **Forbidden**. Later second IG = new profile + unique egress + different role + staggered hours |

`site-caesthetic` `metaPixelId` / `ga4MeasurementId` empty → **no ad spend** until IDs + DEC.

### 2.5 What not to do for “more reach”

- Mass follow / unfollow  
- Identical comments  
- Raise follow cap to chase impressions  
- Buy reach / follower boosts (gray)  
- Clone `@caesthetic.growth` on the same fingerprint  
- Cold DM blast for placement

## 3. Two planes must stay distinct

| Plane | Direction | Primary tools |
|-------|-----------|---------------|
| **Coverage** | We touch *their* accounts | AM/PM timers, story-view, like, follow cap, comment drafts |
| **Placement + amplification** | They see *our* content | Own Stories/Reels/feed, pins, mention loop, save hooks, Email→IG, (later) Meta |

Do not “fix low post views” by raising coverage follow limits.

## 4. 7-day execution order (placement + amplification)

1. Keep follow at 5–8.  
2. Weekday own Stories 1–4 frames (templates).  
3. AM/PM coverage as configured.  
4. Ship 2–3 save-hook feed/Reels from FAQ library.  
5. Fact-comment: drafts on; send only after QA.  
6. Manual mention loop 3–5/day on follow-backs.  
7. Instantly lane per Email→IG when founder GO.  
8. Week end: story reach, profile visits, follow-back %, blocks — only then discuss follow 8→10.
