# CAESTHETIC Entertainment/VOC Topic Bank

## 1. Purpose

E5 is not dependent on the 50 downloaded reference Reels. E5 is the native CAESTHETIC conversation-starter lane built from `CAESTHETIC_OWNER_MARKETING_QUESTIONS.md`.

Its job is to collect owner/operator language that can feed:

- Format A informational Reels;
- Format B synthesis Reels;
- carousels;
- Growth Score FAQ copy;
- objections and sales-language refinement.

Primary KPI: **usable owner/operator verbatim phrases captured per unit**.

## 2. Owner and storage

**VOC bank owner:** Founder  
**Weekly review day:** Friday  
**Activation status:** active.

Founder owns collection, privacy cleaning before storage, storage hygiene and weekly review until a named SMM/community operator is assigned in this file.

Storage rule:

```text
source_surface · post_or_story_id · date · prompt_id · respondent_type · verbatim_phrase · context_note · usable_for · privacy_status · reviewer
```

Default storage target until a Sheet/DB exists:

```text
Dropbox: CAESTHETIC/content/voc/CAESTHETIC_OWNER_VERBATIM_BANK.tsv
```

Do not store patient-identifying facts, health details, treatment details, screenshots of DMs with names, or private profile details in Git.

## 3. Surface-specific measurement

| Surface | VOC mechanism | Count as VOC? | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Feed extra post / Reel prompt | Public comments | Yes, if owner/operator language is usable | Best surface for public verbatim. |
| Stories open question sticker | DM replies | Yes, if manually captured and privacy-cleaned | Use open questions, not only polls. |
| Stories poll with options | Option taps | No, not verbatim | Useful for lightweight direction only. |
| Stories quiz/slider | Tap/slider result | No, not verbatim | Treat as engagement signal only. |
| Comment replies by CAESTHETIC | Follow-up language | Yes, if owner clarifies a problem | Do not pitch in the reply. |

## 4. Cadence

Default month-one cadence:

- **Stories:** two E5 open-question Stories per week, normally Tuesday and Thursday.
- **Feed extra post:** maximum one E5 feed/Reel prompt per week, normally Tuesday, only if it is ready and does not displace evidence production.
- **Wednesday informational Reel:** protected. Entertainment/E5 never fills Wednesday.

If the Wednesday informational Reel is not ready:

1. publish a compliant cutdown from `derived_episodes`, or
2. skip Wednesday.

Do not replace it with entertainment just because entertainment is ready.

## 5. No-numbers rule for entertainment/VOC

E5 and other entertainment-lane content must not contain:

- numbers;
- percentages;
- benchmarks;
- rankings;
- time promises;
- response-time figures;
- revenue, ROI or cost examples;
- pseudo-statistical punchlines.

If a post needs a number, it exits entertainment and must go through the evidence/claim compiler.

## 7. Privacy cleaning before storage

Privacy cleaning happens before a phrase is written into the VOC bank, not after.

Required cleanup:

- remove names, handles, screenshots, profile details and contact details;
- remove any patient-identifying facts;
- remove procedure/treatment details when they could identify a patient or imply medical advice;
- downgrade or discard any phrase that cannot be stored without PHI/compliance risk;
- set `privacy_status` before the row is saved.

Extra caution: E5-Q17 and E5-Q19 can invite clinician-on-camera, trust, review and patient-story replies. Capture only privacy-cleaned owner language about the business problem, not clinical or patient specifics.

## 8. Weekly review

Every week, the named VOC owner reviews E5 output and records:

- prompts used;
- owner/operator replies captured;
- unusable replies and why;
- new phrases added to the verbatim bank;
- topics promoted into Format A;
- any sign of staff-skew or vendor-skew.

If no owner/operator verbatim is captured after two weeks, E5 content should be rewritten before increasing volume.

This rule is active under the Founder/Friday owner assignment above.
