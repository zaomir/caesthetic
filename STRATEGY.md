# CAESTHETIC Strategy

> This file is a short agent-facing strategy index. It is **not** the canonical product SSOT.
> Canonical product truth lives in `docs/ssot/CAESTHETIC.md` and its referenced canonical documents.
> Growth Score execution is additionally governed by `docs/projects/caesthetic/GROWTH_SCORE_AGENT_ENFORCEMENT.md` and its canonical production SOP.

## Purpose

Help aesthetic practices identify the binding constraint on growth, decide what to fix first, avoid funding known leaks, execute a focused intervention, and verify whether the change was adopted and produced measurable impact.

## Positioning

CAESTHETIC is a vertical Growth Intelligence & Control Platform for aesthetic practices. It is not a generic CRM, EHR, ad dashboard, or reporting layer. Its operating loop is:

`Evidence → Constraint → Priority → Decision → Intervention → Adoption → Verified Impact → Learning`

The free Growth Score is the diagnostic entry product. The 30-Day Sprint is the focused execution product that follows an approved diagnosis.

## Users

Primary users are owners and growth decision-makers at aesthetic clinics and related medical-aesthetic practices. Operators and managers may participate in evidence collection and implementation, but binding Growth Score selections and publication gates remain human-controlled where the canonical SOP requires them.

## Boundaries

- Do not fabricate evidence, rankings, patient outcomes, revenue impact, or guarantees.
- Growth Score research uses public/open sources only unless the canonical SOP is explicitly changed.
- Do not bypass Research Alignment, evidence verification, Focus Selection, publication, access, pricing, legal, privacy, or deployment gates.
- Do not treat the lowest surface score as an automatic binding constraint.
- Do not turn CAESTHETIC into a generic CRM/EHR or a dashboard-first product.
- Do not let Compound Engineering artifacts override the canonical SSOT, mandatory agent adapters, or current explicit user instructions.

## Key metrics

Canonical metric definitions live in the product SSOT and Growth Cockpit materials. Strategy-level measures are:

1. Growth Score completion and delivery rate.
2. Share of delivered Growth Scores that progress to an approved intervention/Sprint.
3. Intervention adoption rate.
4. Share of matured interventions with Verified Impact evidence.
5. Reusable learning captured from completed interventions and engineering changes.

## Tracks

1. **Growth Score:** single-location and Multi-Location public-evidence diagnostics with human approval gates.
2. **30-Day Sprint:** execution against one Primary Gap plus Supporting Gaps chosen by the approved workflow.
3. **Growth Cockpit:** decision, intervention, adoption, impact, and learning history.
4. **Agent infrastructure:** deterministic routing, SSOT enforcement, evidence integrity, safe autonomous execution, review, and reusable institutional memory.

## Authority order for agents

When sources disagree, use this order:

1. Current explicit user instruction, unless it requests violating a mandatory safety/policy gate.
2. Mandatory Growth Score enforcement adapter and its canonical production SOP for audit work.
3. Canonical CAESTHETIC SSOT and project-specific agent instructions.
4. `AGENTS.md` / `START.md` routing and repository operating rules.
5. This `STRATEGY.md` summary.
6. Compound Engineering plans, solutions, pulse reports, and other generated artifacts.

If a lower layer conflicts with a higher layer, stop the affected action and resolve the conflict instead of silently choosing the weaker rule.
