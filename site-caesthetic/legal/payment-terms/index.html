Payment Terms

Last updated: September 3, 2026 · OXFORD PROJECTS LTD trading as CAESTHETIC

Scope

This page describes how CAESTHETIC issues payment requests and receives payment. It supplements, and does not replace, the Terms of Use and the payment terms stated in your signed order.

How you pay

A client-specific payment request is issued only after the applicable written order is signed. CAESTHETIC is the seller and source of truth for the order, amount, currency and invoice reference; the named payment provider executes the transfer. CAESTHETIC does not publish an open or reusable provider payment URL. Each private payment link is tied to one order and displays the exact amount due before redirecting to the payment provider.

Exact amount and reference

The amount and reference shown on the CAESTHETIC payment page are the amount and reference that must be paid. Provider screens may prefill these values rather than lock them. Do not alter the amount or reference. A provider redirect or a screenshot does not prove payment; CAESTHETIC verifies the funds actually received against the expected amount, currency and reference.

When payment is received

Payment is treated as received only after confirmed funds are credited to CAESTHETIC and reconciled to the payment request. A missing reference, partial payment, overpayment or other mismatch is placed into manual review and does not automatically activate service. A paid service begins only after credited payment and completion of the access gate stated in the order; the 30-day Sprint period begins on the resulting Sprint Start Date.

Lead-to-Revenue Check credit

If an eligible $500 Lead-to-Revenue Check leads directly into the next CAESTHETIC 30-Day Growth Sprint, CAESTHETIC credits that fee once toward the Sprint's $2,500 total. The remaining Sprint balance is $2,000. Eligibility and the credit appear in the written Sprint Order before payment. The credit is not cash, a refund, a transferable or recurring balance, and it does not apply to unrelated services.

Who may pay

An invoice may be paid from an individual or company account, including a practice owner's personal account. The payer must control the account used and confirm that they are authorized to pay the named invoice on behalf of the contracting practice. CAESTHETIC records the payer's relationship to the practice; it does not represent a personal bank account as a business account.

Payment link security

Private payment links use an opaque token and are not intended to be indexed or shared publicly. The payment-provider base link and provider credentials are not stored in public site code. If a payment request has already been paid, cancelled, refunded, returned or expired, the private payment page will not start a new payment.

Duplicate payment and overpayment

If an invoice is paid more than once, or for more than the invoiced amount, contact info@caesthetic.com. The difference is credited or refunded once the duplicate or excess payment is verified.

Cancellation and refund

Cancellation notice, timing, and the treatment of already-earned fees and any non-cancellable third-party commitments are stated in the applicable order. Work that has been delivered, approved, or already performed is earned and is not undone by a later cancellation. Where the order provides for it, an objectively unperformed and uncured portion of scope may be credited or refunded.

Objection and cure

A timely, specific written objection during the window stated in the applicable order gives CAESTHETIC an opportunity to investigate and cure before any credit is considered.

Billing concerns and disputes

Contact info@caesthetic.com with any billing concern before filing a payment dispute with a bank or card issuer, so CAESTHETIC has a reasonable opportunity to investigate and, where appropriate, cure. This does not limit any right you have to dispute a charge with your bank or card issuer.

What we don't put in a payment record

CAESTHETIC never places card numbers, online-banking credentials, or any patient name, appointment detail, treatment information, or other protected health information in an invoice, payment description, payment reference, or billing evidence record.
