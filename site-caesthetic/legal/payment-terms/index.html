Scope

This page describes how CAESTHETIC records product orders, issues payment requests and receives payment. It supplements, and does not replace, the Terms of Use and the terms in the applicable signed or electronically accepted Order.

How the fixed public products are ordered

The fixed $500 Lead-to-Revenue Check and fixed $2,500 30-Day Growth Sprint may be ordered electronically from their product pages. The secure Order page asks for the practice or business name, purchaser name and work email. By continuing to payment, the purchaser confirms authority to purchase and pay for the named practice/business and electronically accepts the standard product Order, these Payment Terms and the applicable Terms of Use. Product, price and currency are set by CAESTHETIC server-side and are not taken from browser-supplied amounts.

For the Check, internal evidence access is still confirmed before any internal data is shared. For the Sprint, the priority implementation scope, required access/approvals and Sprint Start Date are confirmed before implementation begins. Client-specific Growth System work and other custom scopes may still require a separate written Order or Commercial Schedule before payment.

How you pay

CAESTHETIC is the seller and source of truth for the Order, amount, currency and payment reference; Wise or another approved payment provider executes the transfer. Provider credentials and reusable provider base URLs remain server-side. The provider opens only after a CAESTHETIC Order and Payment Request have been created.

Exact amount and reference

The amount and reference in the CAESTHETIC payment record are the amount and reference that must be paid. Provider screens may prefill these values rather than lock them. Do not alter the amount or reference where the provider allows editing. A provider redirect, browser return, screenshot or payer statement does not prove payment; CAESTHETIC verifies the funds actually received against the expected amount, currency and reference.

When payment is received

Payment is treated as received only after confirmed funds are credited to CAESTHETIC and reconciled to the Payment Request. Until then, a status page may say that payment is being confirmed but may not state that payment was received. A missing reference, partial payment, overpayment or other mismatch is placed into manual review and does not automatically activate service.

After a credited payment for the fixed Check or Sprint, CAESTHETIC will contact the purchaser within 24 hours with the next step. This is a contact/handoff commitment, not a guarantee that implementation begins or produces a business outcome within 24 hours.

Lead-to-Revenue Check credit

If an eligible $500 Lead-to-Revenue Check leads directly into the next qualifying CAESTHETIC 30-Day Growth Sprint, CAESTHETIC credits that fee once toward the Sprint's unchanged $2,500 total. The remaining balance is $2,000. The credit is not cash, a refund, a transferable or recurring balance, and it does not apply to unrelated services.

Who may pay

The purchaser must control or be authorized to use the account used for payment and must be authorized to pay on behalf of the named practice/business. CAESTHETIC does not receive a card number, bank login or online-banking credentials from the product Order form.

Payment link security

CAESTHETIC payment state uses an opaque token tied to one Payment Request. Provider base links and provider credentials are not stored in public site code. A paid, cancelled, refunded, returned or expired Payment Request cannot be treated as a new unpaid Order merely because a provider page can still be opened.

Duplicate payment and overpayment

If an Order is paid more than once, or for more than the expected amount, contact info@caesthetic.com. The difference is credited or refunded once the duplicate or excess payment is verified.

Cancellation and refund

Cancellation notice, timing, and the treatment of already-earned fees and any non-cancellable third-party commitments are stated in the applicable Order. Work that has been delivered, approved, or already performed is earned and is not undone by a later cancellation. Where the Order provides for it, an objectively unperformed and uncured portion of scope may be credited or refunded.

Objection and cure

A timely, specific written objection during the window stated in the applicable Order gives CAESTHETIC an opportunity to investigate and cure before any credit is considered.

Billing concerns and disputes

Contact info@caesthetic.com with a billing concern before filing a payment dispute with a bank or card issuer, so CAESTHETIC has a reasonable opportunity to investigate and, where appropriate, cure. This does not limit any right you have to dispute a charge with your bank or card issuer.

What we don't put in a payment record

CAESTHETIC never places card numbers, online-banking credentials, patient names, appointment details, treatment information or other protected health information in an Order payment description, payment reference or billing evidence record.
