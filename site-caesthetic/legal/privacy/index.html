Health information and payment records

CAESTHETIC does not place patient names, appointment details, treatment information, medical information or other protected health information in invoices, payment references, payment-provider metadata, billing evidence or payment-support messages. Payment records are limited to the commercial relationship between CAESTHETIC and the practice or its authorized payer.

The Lead-to-Revenue Check is designed around least-privilege, non-clinical evidence. Practices should not provide unnecessary patient names, medical records, treatment details or protected health information. The applicable written scope and secure access method govern any permitted internal evidence; passwords and access credentials should never be sent by email.

We use personal data to respond to Score requests, deliver reports, perform an authorized Lead-to-Revenue Check, issue and reconcile payment requests, record payer authorization, send payment reminders and receipts, operate the site, measure performance, and send service communications. Marketing email is sent only with a clear unsubscribe path.

We will verify the request and respond within the time required by law. You will not be discriminated against for exercising these rights.
