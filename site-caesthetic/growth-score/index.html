Growth Score

Find the constraint before you fund more activity.

Growth Score is a practice-specific diagnostic across Search, Website, Social and Reputation. It shows what is objectively strong, where demand breaks, what to fix first and what not to spend on yet. It is free; delivery timing is confirmed after request against current analysis capacity.

What is measured

Four surfaces. One separate consistency check.

  • Search / GBP · 30%Map visibility, treatment and category completeness, identity, conversion readiness, freshness and branded-search control.
  • Website · 25%Booking friction, treatment clarity, mobile performance, above-fold conversion, trust proof and booking integrity.
  • Social · 15%Treatment presence, clinician expertise, proof quality, recency, profile-to-booking continuity and local offer clarity.
  • Reputation · 30%90-day review velocity, rating, depth, recency, responses, negative-review handling and treatment proof.
  • Cross-SurfaceTreatment, positioning, proof, conversion and identity consistency. Reported separately, never treated as a fifth surface.

If less than 70% of a surface's metric weight is final, that surface reads “Insufficient evidence.” Available metrics are reweighted inside that surface; the Overall score is secondary to the diagnosis.

AI-assisted, human-verified

Software organizes evidence. A person verifies the diagnosis.

AI handles evidence collection, normalization, comparison and preliminary component scoring. The output is AI-assisted, human approved after verification. A human verifies observable facts, approves judgment metrics, identifies the objective strength and binding constraint, and selects the Top 3 priorities plus one “do not do.”

Published findings rely primarily on observable Class A evidence. At least 80% of published findings are Class A. Any Class B assumptions are explicit, include their basis and are never presented as verified outcomes.

What happens next

A short request, then a private Score page.

  • 01
    You submit four required fields in two short stages. No revenue or budget questions.
  • 02
    Your request is safely recorded before we offer any optional context questions.
  • 03
    AI-assisted collection builds the evidence file; a human verifies it and approves the diagnosis.
  • 04
    You receive a private, noindex /score/ page and a 3–8 minute human-recorded walkthrough.
  • 05
    If useful, the optional next step is the fixed-price 30-Day Growth Sprint. The Score does not promise its scope in advance.

Cost of the Growth Score: $0. No guaranteed patient, ranking, or revenue outcome.
