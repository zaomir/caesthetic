Owner FAQ

What owners usually need clarified.

Do I need to buy more traffic first?

No. The Growth Score identifies whether Search, Website, Social, Reputation or the conversion path is the binding constraint before additional activity is funded.

Can I stop after the Sprint?

Yes. There is no required retainer, and delivered materials remain with the practice.

Do brand assets need to be ready?

No. Existing identity, clinician proof, permissions and available source assets are checked during diagnosis. Missing or unapproved assets are treated as a visible dependency, not silently fabricated.

Is every monthly workstream included?

No. The Fixed Management Fee covers the signed recurring ownership scope inside the Growth Budget. Monthly priorities follow evidence and practice capacity; approved external inputs use the variable balance or an approved top-up.
