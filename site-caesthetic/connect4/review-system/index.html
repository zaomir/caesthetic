Understanding inside.

We implement our own post-visit review and feedback system. It helps clients share their experience and gives leaders a clearer view of what is happening at each location and which issues need attention.

01 / The need

Make good work visible. Make problems actionable.

An average rating alone cannot explain the quality of your service. Leaders need specific signals: what a client valued, where something went wrong and who is following up.

Our system combines consistent, genuine feedback collection with an organized process for private concerns. We adapt our software to your location and hand over a defined team workflow.

For the client

An easier way to share their experience

A clear way to leave a review or contact the team. The client decides what to say and where to share it.

For the leader

A concern has an owner

An operational log shows which concerns have been received, taken into work and closed by the team.

02 / Implementation

Our system, configured for your location.

01Feedback collection
Configure location details, agreed entry points and a clear invitation to share a genuine experience.
02Private concerns
Clients can describe a concern. The responsible team member receives a notification and appropriate details under the agreed access rules.
03Follow-up visibility
The team works from a log with clear statuses. Leaders can see recurring themes and unresolved concerns.
04Team handover
Name an owner, provide instructions and verify the agreed scenarios with test data before launch. Check whether the team uses the process after launch.

03 / Your deliverables

A working process and a way to check adoption.

  • A configured system for the agreed location.
  • Verified links to suitable public review platforms.
  • Notifications for the responsible person and access to the operational log.
  • A response and follow-up guide.
  • Launch verification and clear ongoing team responsibilities.

Integrations, invitation channels, hosting and ongoing support are defined in the proposal. We preserve an effective existing process when another system is unnecessary.

04 / Our principle

Reputation starts with a real experience.

Public review access remains available regardless of a client’s rating. Private feedback helps the team understand a concern while the client remains free to share their experience publicly.

We do not prescribe review wording or promise a particular rating. The system supports consistent feedback collection and attention to the causes of dissatisfaction. Rating changes and verified outcomes are measured separately.

Questions

Before we start.

Is this your own software?

Yes. We use our own feedback collection and follow-up system and adapt it to the agreed client workflow. Capabilities and launch conditions are defined in the scope.

What happens to negative feedback?

Private concerns reach the team for follow-up. Leaders can see their content, themes and status. Public reviews remain under the control of their authors and the relevant platform.

Does the system guarantee a high rating?

No. Ratings reflect real experiences and client decisions. We are accountable for system setup, appropriate invitations and team handover.

Can we start with one location?

Yes. We launch the agreed process at one location, check adoption and then extend it to other locations as appropriate.

Next step

Make feedback part of everyday operations.

Start with one location, its current process and a responsible contact. We will define what to implement, what to preserve and how to verify the launch.
