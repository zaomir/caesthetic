01 / The problem

Conflicting details make the next step harder.

Your website shows one phone number and Maps another. A service appears on the network website without saying which office provides it. A review or booking link opens a different city.

These are observable, repairable issues. Their effect on inquiries needs separate measurement.

Before · illustrative example

The patient has to work it out

  • Different opening hours, without an explanation.
  • A network service with no confirmed local availability.
  • A link that opens another location.

After · implementation outcome

The details lead to the right place

  • Office hours and appointment conditions are clear.
  • Services and providers are confirmed for the location.
  • Contact, booking and review links reach the correct destination.

02 / The work

Four surfaces. One agreed source of facts.

01Search & Maps
Check names, addresses, phone numbers, hours, categories, services and links on existing profiles. Distinguish the network, local partner and individual practitioner before correcting confirmed errors.
02Website & blog
Update the selected location and service pages and create useful content that answers client questions. Connect each service with its actual location, provider and next step.
03Social
Align profile details, selected local posts and links. Use the relevant network or partner account; every office does not automatically need a separate account.
04Reviews & Reputation
Select platforms relevant to the practice, identify the correct entity and check its local review link. Update accessible details and prepare useful business replies.

Alignment means accurate facts and a consistent meaning. Content fits each platform. Services, providers, phone numbers and availability can legitimately differ by location.

03 / Your deliverables

See exactly what changed.

  • An agreed location information register.
  • Published pages, content and profile updates within the written scope.
  • A before-and-after record with links and verification dates.
  • A separate list of submissions still awaiting platform moderation.
  • Clear responsibilities for keeping services, teams and opening hours current.

When feedback collection is in scope, we implement our review and feedback system. Launch and team adoption are verified separately.

04 / Organic visibility

Build the foundation without buying advertising.

Accurate details, useful local pages and coherent links help people and search systems understand your offer. We also check that the selected website pages are technically accessible for indexing.

Google considers relevance, distance and prominence. We are accountable for the agreed changes and measure visibility in Search, Maps and AI search separately. Rankings and inclusion in AI answers are not guaranteed.

Questions

Before we start.

Can we start with one location?

Yes. We define the observed issues, priority service, platforms and acceptance criteria first. Your proposal states the price and scope. After the first location, you decide whether to continue.

What does our team need to provide?

A responsible contact, confirmed facts and access to the agreed pages and profiles. Clinical content is confirmed by the practice.

Will you merge different listings or business names?

Only when their roles, ownership and the permitted changes have been checked. A network and a local practice can legitimately use different names and phone numbers.

Do we have to replace our marketer?

No. We can work with your team. You keep the completed materials and update instructions after the agreed engagement.
