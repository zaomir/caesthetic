Why you may have heard from us

A relevant public signal, then a permission question.

CAESTHETIC may contact owners, doctors and other legitimate business decision-makers at independent aesthetic practices when a current, publicly verifiable signal makes the Growth Score relevant.

The opening email should identify that public signal and ask whether you want the free Growth Score. Receiving an email does not mean that CAESTHETIC has already completed a diagnosis or accessed your internal systems.

Outside-in boundary

We start with the four public surfaces patients can inspect.

  • Search / Google Business ProfileHow the practice appears when local patients search and compare.
  • WebsiteWhether the public site explains the offer, proof and next step clearly.
  • SocialWhether current public content supports trust and routes attention coherently.
  • Reputation / ReviewsWhat visible review evidence and owner responses communicate at the decision point.

From public evidence alone, we do not claim to know your CRM, call handling, reception performance, patient data, revenue, close rate or internal conversion constraint. Those areas remain not assessed unless the practice separately authorizes appropriate access.
