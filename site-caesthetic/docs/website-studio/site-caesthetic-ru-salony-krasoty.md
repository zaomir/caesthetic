## Design discovery

**DESIGN DISCOVERY:** assumptions recorded from approved canon; Russian copy is a standalone localized decision surface.

- Audience: Russian-speaking owners of salons and small salon groups.
- Desired action: request the free Salon Growth Score after understanding the method.
- Brand attributes: evidence-led, calm, editorial, precise and owner-focused.
- Anti-attributes: spa-pink visuals, stock models, generic agency promises, channel packages, gradients, fake proof and guarantees.
- Signature idea: the client route from discovery to repeat visit, with one illustrative leak and an evidence ledger.
- Hard constraints: exactly four surfaces; paid demand is not a fifth surface; internal causes stay unassessed without access; public pricing is generated.
