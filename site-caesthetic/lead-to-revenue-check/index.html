What is mapped

Eight stages, with evidence and uncertainty kept separate.

  1. 01Lead receivedSource, timestamp and ownership where available.
  2. 02ResponseWhether and how the practice responds.
  3. 03QualificationThe next-step rules actually used.
  4. 04BookingCompletion, friction and handoff evidence.
  5. 05ConfirmationReminder and confirmation sequence.
  6. 06ShowAttendance status where legitimately available.
  7. 07ConsultationCommercial process only; no clinical judgment.
  8. 08PaymentAggregated commercial completion evidence.

A missing stage is reported as insufficient evidence. Absence of data is never converted into a failure, a lost patient or a revenue claim.

FAQ

What practice owners usually ask.

Do I need this after every Growth Score?

No. If public evidence already confirms the priority constraint, the next justified path may be a Sprint, an in-house fix or no paid work.

Does the Check promise more bookings or revenue?

No. It produces an evidence-backed decision about the permitted internal path. Outcomes depend on implementation, adoption, market conditions and other factors outside the diagnostic.

Will you need clinical records?

No. The intended scope is non-clinical. CAESTHETIC requests the minimum access needed and excludes unnecessary protected health information.

Can my team implement the findings?

Yes. The report identifies the first justified fix and a verification path; ownership remains your decision.
