Performance Fee

Separate above the Growth Budget, client-specific and payable only on verified positive growth under the signed measurement or attribution rule. It activates only through a signed client-specific Commercial Schedule; otherwise it is not activated.

Unused variable funds roll forward as the client's growth balance. The earned Fixed Management Fee does not roll over. No recurring amount, nationwide percentage or cap is published or implied.

Useful work gets a clear route.

A new request is classified as an Included Optimization, Growth Budget Cost, Growth Add-on or Practice Operations Add-on. Urgency does not silently expand the fixed management scope, and adjacency is not a reason to reject useful work.
