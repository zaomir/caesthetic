Four surfaces where demand can break.

  • Search · 30%Patients searching local aesthetic intent never see the practice in the places that produce visits.
  • Website · 25%Too many clicks to book, weak mobile speed, or a phone number that is hard to find.
  • Social · 15%Current work and proof are missing or fail to route attention to the practice.
  • Reputation · 30%Review velocity and reply quality lag named competitors, so shortlists form without you.

Cross-Surface Consistency checks whether positioning, proof, identity and the booking path agree across the four surfaces. It is a separate metric, not a fifth surface.
