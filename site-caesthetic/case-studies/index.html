How we work

Build a path from local search to patient inquiries.

Connect4 synchronizes four public surfaces with Lead Intake to help your practice attract and handle inquiries from organic search and Maps, without relying on paid advertising.

How we keep it connected

Patients may discover you in one place and check another before getting in touch. Each surface has a different job. The information should tell the same story.

  1. Start with specific patient searches

    We map specific, lower-volume searches about your services, locations and patient questions. We connect those searches to the treatments you actually offer and confirm the facts with your team.

  2. Build a consistent answer across all four surfaces

    That search map guides service pages and blog articles, Maps profiles, social posts, and the comments and replies your practice writes. Each format answers the relevant question naturally and offers a clear next step.

  3. Keep the content and the real experience aligned

    One accountable lead coordinates the specialists and keeps the shared search and content map current. We compare published information with real patient feedback, correct inconsistencies and check the handoff into Lead Intake.

Make honest reviews part of the process

We set up neutral invitations to honest reviews and useful, privacy-conscious practice replies. Real reviews and social conversations help us understand patient language and improve business-owned content. Patients choose their own words and rating; we do not script reviews, require keywords, offer incentives or invite only satisfied patients.

Carry the inquiry through to a booking

Lead Intake is what happens after someone reaches out. We review how calls, forms and messages reach your team, who responds, and what happens when a patient does not book. Then we agree the fixes and follow-up process with your team.

An inquiry is not yet a confirmed appointment.

More time for patients. Less day-to-day administration.

When your practice needs broader support, we can also set up patient-feedback systems, automate inquiry routing and follow-up, support front-desk recruitment and training, organize call-center workflows, and coordinate documentation and consent processes with your legal advisers. We agree these services separately, with clear owners and reporting, so less day-to-day administration falls back on the doctor.
